# Constraint and Displacement

A minimal audio interface presenting three essays on structural persistence, abstraction, and institutional drift. The project pairs a deliberately austere visual surface with dynamic audio-reactive rendering, emphasizing constraint rather than ornament.

The three included recordings are:

**Articulatory Ease in the Arabic Script**
An examination of motor continuity, discriminability, and structural tradeoffs in writing systems. The essay analyzes how scripts negotiate the tension between articulatory efficiency and perceptual clarity.

**Computing Does Not Destroy Difficulty**
A structural argument that abstraction redistributes rather than eliminates burden. The piece formalizes the conservation and displacement of difficulty across layers of representation.

**The Broken Internet**
An analysis of gradient extraction and platform decay. The essay interprets digital systems as optimization engines whose unconstrained descent produces institutional and epistemic erosion.
