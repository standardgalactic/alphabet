# Entropy Flux Metrics

These sources collectively introduce and elaborate on the **Relativistic Scalar Vector Plenum (RSVP)** framework—a theoretical model proposing that cognitive and physical phenomena can be understood through the dynamic interplay of three fundamental fields:
- **Scalar field (Φ):** Represents semantic density or potential.
- **Vector field (v⃗):** Corresponds to attention or information flow.
- **Entropy field (S):** Encodes uncertainty or ambiguity.

## Overview of Sources

### 1. [Amplitwist Operators](https://github.com/standardgalactic/antivenom/blob/main/Amplitwist%20Operators.pdf)
Applies RSVP to neuroscience, hypothesizing that cortical columns act as "amplitwist operators" performing local rotation-scaling transformations on neural representations. This enables universal semantic function approximation.

### 2. [Emergent Structures](https://github.com/standardgalactic/antivenom/blob/main/Emergent%20Structures.pdf)
Broadens RSVP’s scope, introducing TARTAN (Trajectory-Aware Recursive Tiling with Annotated Noise) to explain emergent structures and control mechanisms across neural, cosmic, and artificial intelligence systems. Interprets cosmic structure formation as "Expyrotic reintegration" of information.

### 3. [Expyrotic Cosmology](https://github.com/standardgalactic/antivenom/blob/main/Expyrotic%20Cosmology.pdf)
Details a non-inflationary cosmological model, describing how cosmic structure emerges from the reintegration of decohered CMB information over long timescales. Presents RSVP as a thermodynamically consistent alternative to traditional Big Bang theories.

### 4. [Mathematical Structures](https://github.com/standardgalactic/kitbash/blob/main/Mathematical%20Structures.pdf)
Comprehensively expounds RSVP’s mathematical foundations, including field dynamics, metrics of coherence, and applications in cognitive science, narrative analysis, and cinematic visualization.

### 5. [RSVP-ADAPTER](https://github.com/standardgalactic/kitbash/blob/main/RSVP-ADAPTER.pdf)
Integrates RSVP with the ADAPTER model of analogical retrieval, proposing that emotions are control-state dynamics resulting from field reconfigurations triggered by relational mismatches.

---

## Summary

The RSVP framework unifies neuroscience, cosmology, and artificial intelligence via a tripartite field model:
- **Semantic density (Φ), attention flow (v⃗), and entropy (S)** interact to encode and process information.
- RSVP and its extensions (like TARTAN) provide mechanisms for universal semantic function approximation, emergent structure formation, and control-state dynamics.
- The framework’s cosmological implications reinterpret structure formation as informational reintegration rather than inflation.
