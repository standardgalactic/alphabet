Answer Key

1. The central argument is that aphantasia (no mental imagery) and anendophasia (no inner speech) are not cognitive deficits but stable, alternative regimes of cognition. The RSVP model posits that thought is fundamentally a direct traversal of semantic geometry, and that phenomenological rendering like images or inner speech are optional projections, not requirements for computation.
2. The three core RSVP fields are the Scalar Potential (Φ), representing semantic intensity; the Vector Flow (⊑ or v), representing directed cognitive currents; and the Entropy Density (S), representing configurational complexity. Their evolution is governed by a set of coupled partial differential equations that describe how meaning, direction, and complexity interact.
3. The RSVP model formalizes Cox's Mimetic Hypothesis by defining an embodied component of the vector flow (⊑emb or vemb) that couples to the semantic potential (Φ). This allows covert motor simulations (Mimetic Motor Imagery) to channel and sustain semantic computation, providing an alternative pathway for thought when sensory projections are suppressed.
4. CLIO (Cognitive Loop via In-Situ Optimization) functions as an adaptive control system that recursively optimizes cognitive parameters, such as oscillatory gains and damping coefficients. It minimizes a free-energy-like objective, with its gain (κCLIO) regulating the threshold for generating vivid sensory projections, thus sustaining imageless thought at subcritical gain levels.
5. Environmental Stigmergy is the formalization of the extended mind, where cognition couples with the environment. The model uses coupled PDEs to govern the interaction between an internal scalar field (Φint) and an external one (Φext), modeling how artifacts like diagrams or notes function as a read/write memory buffer that stabilizes thought.
6. TARTAN (Trajectory-Aware Recursive Tiling with Annotated Noise) is a multiscale geometry tool that partitions the cognitive manifold into hierarchical tiles. It analyzes local entropy tensors at each scale; the convergence and alignment of these tensors' principal directions across scales signal the emergence of coherent, vivid imagery, while their divergence preserves the imageless semantic regime.
7. Mimetic projection layers (πV, πA) are mathematical operators that project the core semantic field (Φ) into sensory formats, such as visual or auditory imagery. They are considered optional and not part of the governing equations of thought, meaning their suppression does not impair the underlying semantic computation. This principle is referred to as "semantic sufficiency."
8. Amplitwistor modes are mathematical representations of synchronized oscillatory patterns on submanifolds of the cognitive space. They are modulated by midbrain structures like Central Pattern Generators (CPGs), providing a formal mechanism for how different brain regions entrain and communicate. Their stability criteria determine whether sensory modes persist or decay into an imageless state.
9. Quantum SpherePop is a quantum field formulation of RSVP that discretizes cognitive microstates onto a 5D complex-phase lattice, providing a statistical-mechanical foundation for the theory. It extends the classical framework by introducing operator fields (Φ̂, v̂, Ŝ), a quantum Hamiltonian, and concepts like phase synchronization and decoherence to explain cognitive coherence and uncertainty.
10. The prediction of "stigmergic equalization" is that providing external aids like diagrams during a spatial reasoning task will significantly reduce or eliminate performance differences in reaction time and accuracy between aphantasic individuals and controls. The framework predicts this intervention will produce an effect size of d < 0.1.

Glossary of Key Terms

Term	Definition
Adjunction (Write-Read)	A categorical formalization of stigmergic coupling where the "Write" functor (externalizing internal states) and the "Read" functor (internalizing external information) form an adjoint pair, representing an energetically optimal information transfer.
Amplitwistor Modes	Mathematical objects describing synchronized, oscillatory patterns on submanifolds of the cognitive space. Modulated by midbrain activity, they provide a mechanism for neural entrainment and their stability determines the persistence of sensory imagery.
Anendophasia	The condition of absent or significantly reduced inner speech.
Aphantasia	The condition of absent or significantly reduced voluntary mental imagery.
Bures Distance	A metric in quantum information geometry that quantifies the distinguishability between two quantum states (density operators). Geodesics in this metric represent optimal, least-distorting transitions between cognitive states.
CLIO (Cognitive Loop via In-Situ Optimization)	An adaptive control system that adjusts cognitive parameters (e.g., oscillatory gains, damping) to minimize a free-energy-like objective. The "CLIO gain" (κCLIO) regulates the vividness of mental projections.
Cox's Mimetic Hypothesis	The theory that perception, understanding, and imagination are grounded in covert motor simulations (mimetic motor processes) that proxy for perception and action.
CPTP Maps	Completely Positive Trace-Preserving maps. In the quantum RSVP model, CLIO updates are formalized as CPTP maps acting on local density fields, modeling adaptive inference steps.
Ehrenfest Dynamics	A theorem in quantum mechanics stating that the expectation values of quantum operators evolve according to classical laws of motion. It is used to demonstrate how the classical RSVP field equations emerge as the semiclassical limit of the quantum model.
Embodied Stigmergy	The process of using the external environment (e.g., diagrams, notes, tools) as an interactive memory store through physical action. It is modeled by coupling internal and external RSVP fields.
Environmental Stigmergy	See Embodied Stigmergy.
Functorial Quantization	A categorical approach to formalizing the relationship between classical and quantum RSVP. A "quantization functor" maps classical field configurations to quantum Hilbert spaces and classical evolutions to quantum channels (CPTP maps).
Kuramoto Order Parameter (r)	A measure of phase synchronization among a population of oscillators, ranging from 0 (incoherent) to 1 (fully synchronized). It is used to quantify cognitive coherence.
λ-Calculus (Linear)	A formal system of computation used to provide an operational semantics for RSVP cognition. In this interpretation, β-reduction (computation) corresponds to local entropic relaxation of the fields.
