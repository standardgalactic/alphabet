# Briefing Document

## Executive Summary

This briefing synthesizes a unified theoretical framework presented across a series of documents that offers a radical reconceptualization of cognition, language, intelligence, and even fundamental physics. The central argument is a rejection of prevailing state-based, representational, and optimization-centric models in favor of an event-first ontology. In this view, the fundamental reality of any adaptive system is its construction history—an irreversible, append-only log of events. States are merely derivative, partial projections of this history.

The framework establishes **counterfactual completeness** as the definitive criterion for scientific explanation, moving beyond mere prediction or correlation. A theory is explanatory only if it can specify which structural invariants are preserved under structured, counterfactual interventions. This principle is used to mount a structural critique of contemporary statistical models like Large Language Models (LLMs), which are characterized as *surface projection optimizers* that lack the architectural capacity for genuine causal reasoning.

Cognition and language are reinterpreted through the lens of perceptual control theory. The brain does not represent abstract structures like syntax; it acts to maintain them as invariants over event histories. This control process is implemented via neural oscillatory dynamics: gamma activity generates speculative proposals, phase synchronization serves as an arbiter to enforce causal order, and cross-frequency coupling implements invariant-preserving collapse. Abstract linguistic properties, such as nonassociativity, are shown to emerge from irreversible *commit* events in this control architecture rather than from symbolic rules.

This entire system is proposed to run on a minimal computational substrate, **Spherepop OS**, defined by an authoritative event log, deterministic replay, and invariant-preserving collapse. At a broader philosophical level, the framework defines intelligence as *construction under constraint*, where core operations include modularity for preserving local coherence and **refusal**—the structural inability to perform an invalid action—as a constitutive feature, not a failure.

Finally, the event-first ontology is extended to fundamental physics, proposing that proper time is not a primitive dimension but a measure of the cumulative density of irreversible, constraint-imposing events along a worldline.

## Core Thesis: Event-First Ontology and Counterfactual Explanation

### Critique of State-Based and Statistical Models

State-based explanations collapse history, erase order, and discard the contingencies required for lawful generalization. Two systems may occupy identical states yet differ profoundly in robustness and transfer capacity due to divergent construction histories.

Statistical models—especially LLMs—operate on surface projections of generative processes. They minimize prediction error but lack access to causal structure, intervention semantics, or invariant enforcement. As such, they are **counterfactually incomplete**: they cannot answer what would remain invariant under structured perturbation.

> Without arrows, there are no counterfactuals; without counterfactuals, there is no explanation.

### The Primacy of Event Histories

Systems are defined by the space of histories they admit, not by the states they temporarily occupy. Events are irreversible and accumulate constraints. Memory is replayability; identity is path-dependent.

### Explanation as Counterfactual Completeness

An explanation is valid only if it specifies:
1. A constrained history space  
2. Intervention operators  
3. Deterministic replay  
4. Equivalence relations defining invariants  

Systems lacking these are explanatorily incomplete by construction.

### Time as Constraint Accumulation

Proper time is proportional to irreversible event density, not a fundamental dimension. Time dilation reflects suppressed event rates; equilibrium corresponds to the exhaustion of time-generating processes.

### Intelligence as Construction Under Constraint
