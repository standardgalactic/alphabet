\chapter*{Introduction to Part IV}
\addcontentsline{toc}{chapter}{Introduction to Part IV}
\markboth{Introduction to Part IV}{Introduction to Part IV}

Part~IV is where the coarsening examples of Part~III become
a genuine field theory.
The central methodological commitment is:

\principle{
  No geometric claim is accepted unless licensed by analysis.\\[4pt]
  \textbf{Field Equations} $\Rightarrow$ \textbf{Regularity}
  $\Rightarrow$ \textbf{Sobolev Category}
  $\Rightarrow$ \textbf{Admissibility Sheaf}
  $\Rightarrow$ \textbf{Cohomology.}
}

\begin{tcolorbox}[title={\textbf{Established So Far}},
  colback=boxgreen,colframe=green!50!black,fonttitle=\small\sffamily]
From Part~III: $\frac{d\mathcal{F}}{dt} \leq 0$ for gradient flows;
Allen--Cahn ($z=2$) and Cahn--Hilliard ($z=3$) scaling;
topological defects as obstructions to coarsening.
From Part~II: reconstruction error $E(x) = d(x, G(F(x)))$;
equivalence classes; adjoint reconstruction.
\end{tcolorbox}

\bigskip

\begin{tcolorbox}[title={\textbf{New Ideas in This Part}},
  colback=boxblue,colframe=blue!40!black,fonttitle=\small\sffamily]
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=1.5em,itemsep=2pt]
  \item The RSVP free-energy functional
    $\mathcal{F}[\Phi,\mathbf{v},S,\ell]$.
  \item Lamphron $L = \ell^2 \geq 0$ (positivity by construction).
  \item Lamphrodyne $D = -\delta\mathcal{F}/\delta S$
    (derived, not postulated).
  \item All four variational derivatives of $\mathcal{F}$.
  \item RSVP gradient-flow equations and their dissipation theorem.
  \item Conservative lamphron variant (Cahn--Hilliard type).
  \item Sobolev regularity hierarchy: $H^1$ (Allen--Cahn),
    $H^2$ (Cahn--Hilliard energy), $H^4$ (classical CH).
  \item Dimensionless parameters $Pe$, $\Gamma$, $Ro_{\mathrm{RSVP}}$.
  \item Conjectured RSVP coarsening phase diagram.
\end{itemize}
\end{tcolorbox}
