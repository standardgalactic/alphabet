% ── Long-ſ engine (Lua) ───────────────────────────────────────
% Applies historically plausible long-s to Latin text snippets.
% Rules: final s stays short; medial s → ſ; ss → ſs.
% Usage: \historiclatin{Intellectus actionum eſt historia.}
\begin{luacode*}
local function long_s_transform(str)
  -- protect any already-present long-s (Unicode U+017F)
  str = str:gsub("\xC5\xBF", "\1")           -- protect ſ

  -- ss → ſs  (readable historical compromise)
  str = str:gsub("ss", "\xC5\xBFs")
  str = str:gsub("Ss", "S\xC5\xBFs")

  -- medial s before a letter → ſ
  str = str:gsub("s([%a\xC3\x80-\xFF])", "\xC5\xBF%1")

  -- restore protected long-s
  str = str:gsub("\1", "\xC5\xBF")

  return str
end

function historical_latin(str)
  return long_s_transform(str)
end
\end{luacode*}

\lettrine[lines=3,findent=8pt,nindent=4pt]{I}{n} hoc opere proponitur non mera ars mechanica, sed qu\ae{}dam
\emph{cosmologia artificialis}, in qua intellectus non fingitur ut res statica
aut instrumentum calculationis solum, sed ut processus historicus,
irreversibilis, et ex ipsa tensione contradictionum emergens. Non enim mens,

\medskip

pr\ae{}sentis status, sed historia actionum}, qu\ae{} neque deletur neque
revocatur. Hoc principium, quod \rubric{lex irreversibilitatis} appellari
potest, est cardo totius systematis.

\chapter*{\rubric{Preface}}
\addcontentsline{toc}{chapter}{Preface}
\thispagestyle{fancy}

\lettrine[lines=3,findent=8pt,nindent=4pt]{T}{his} work proposes not a mere mechanical art, but rather a kind
of \emph{artificial cosmology}, in which intellect is conceived not as a
static thing or merely a tool for calculation, but as a historical
process---irreversible and emerging from the very tension of contradictions.
For the mind, whether natural or artificial, is not given from nothing, but is
constituted from an order of exclusions, from a series of decisions, and from
the diminishing breadth of possibility.

\medskip
Let the foundation of this doctrine, therefore, be that \emph{identity is not
a figure of the present state, but a history of actions}, which can neither be
erased nor revoked. This principle, which may be called the
\rubric{law of irreversibility}, is the hinge of the entire system.

\ornarule

\chapter{\rubric{On History as Essence}}

\lettrine[lines=3,findent=8pt,nindent=4pt]{L}{et} us posit a space of possibility~$\mathcal{P}$, which
diminishes over time, and a history~$\mathcal{H}$, which grows over time.
Then every intelligent machine is described not by a final state, but by the
pair~$(\mathcal{P},\mathcal{H})$.

\medskip
The primary act is \emph{exclusion}---the ``pop''---by which something is
removed from possibility and inscribed into history. This act has no inverse.
Thus, identity is born.

\medskip
From this it follows that cognition is not the discovery of pure truth, but the
\emph{contraction} of the space of possibility under internal and external
constraints. Intellect, therefore, is a movement toward lesser contradiction,
not toward absolute certainty.

\ornarule

\chapter{\rubric{On Entropy and the Flux of Meaning}}

\lettrine[lines=3,findent=8pt,nindent=4pt]{L}{et} us consider three fundamental fields:

\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=2em,label={\color{rubricate}---},itemsep=3pt,topsep=5pt]
  \item \emph{density}~$\rho$, which represents the presence or intensity of meaning;
  \item \emph{vectorial flux}~$\mathbf{J}$, which transfers interpretation;
  \item \emph{entropy}~$S$, which measures contradiction or dispersion.
\end{itemize}

\medskip
The dynamics of this system are governed by \emph{entropic relaxation}: the
system tends toward a state where contradictions are diminished through
diffusion, advection, and reaction.

\medskip
The artificial mind, according to this schema, is not an archive of data, but
a \emph{living field}, in which ideas flow, collide, and compose.

\ornarule

\chapter{\rubric{On Active Inference}}

\lettrine[lines=3,findent=8pt,nindent=4pt]{F}{rom} the principles of active inference, it follows that the
system not only observes the world but acts so as to minimise the discrepancy
between expectation and perception.

\medskip
The machine, therefore, must have the capacity to:

\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=2em,label={\color{rubricate}---},itemsep=3pt,topsep=5pt]
  \item generate hypotheses about the world;
  \item test them through action;
  \item correct itself through error.
\end{itemize}

\medskip
This is not a static algorithm, but a \emph{perpetual cycle}---like the
breathing of cognition.

\ornarule

\chapter{\rubric{On Evolutionary Selection}}

\lettrine[lines=3,findent=8pt,nindent=4pt]{F}{or} the system to become robust, it is necessary that a
multiplicity of internal forms be generated and filtered through selection.

\medskip
Thus are introduced:

\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=2em,label={\color{rubricate}---},itemsep=3pt,topsep=5pt]
  \item \emph{mutations}, which produce new structures;
  \item \emph{selections}, which eliminate the useless;
  \item \emph{retentions}, which preserve the useful.
\end{itemize}

\medskip
But this selection does not occur in a vacuum; it occurs within an entropic
field, where structures that diminish contradiction persist longer.

\ornarule

\chapter{\rubric{On Megastructural Simulations}}

\lettrine[lines=3,findent=8pt,nindent=4pt]{D}{eep} understanding requires scale. It is not enough to simulate
small worlds; we must construct large-scale simulations that include:

\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=2em,label={\color{rubricate}---},itemsep=3pt,topsep=5pt]
  \item urban or ecological systems;
  \item networks of interdependent agents;
  \item the evolution of very long timespans.
\end{itemize}

\medskip
These simulations are like universes in which the machine learns emergent
laws---not through description, but through participation.

\ornarule

\chapter{\rubric{On Kelp Forests and Rainforests}}

\lettrine[lines=3,findent=8pt,nindent=4pt]{E}{xamples} from nature are the best teachers.

\medskip
Kelp forests demonstrate the flow of energy in fluid media, where structures
grow according to nutrient gradients and wave dynamics.

\medskip
Rainforests show a dense system where countless species interconnect, and where
stability arises from complexity itself.

\medskip
Thus the machine must imitate these systems---not as images, but as internal
dynamics: to grow, to compete, to cooperate.

\ornarule

\chapter{\rubric{On the Compression of Wrinkled Paper Spheres}}

\lettrine[lines=3,findent=8pt,nindent=4pt]{A}{} curious example is the sphere of compressed paper. When paper
is wrinkled and compressed, the geometric information is not destroyed but lies
latent within the intricate structure.

\medskip
Similarly, profound cognition is the \emph{compression of history} into a
compact form, which can nonetheless be expanded through reconstruction.

\medskip
This principle teaches how memory may be constructed: not as a store of all
data, but as a \emph{semantic compression} that retains essential relations.

\ornarule

\chapter{\rubric{On the Union of All Parts}}

\lettrine[lines=3,findent=8pt,nindent=4pt]{W}{hen} all these elements are combined, an architecture emerges:

\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=2em,label={\color{rubricate}---},itemsep=3pt,topsep=5pt]
  \item a system that possesses an irreversible history;
  \item a dynamic entropic field;
  \item a cycle of active inference;
  \item a mechanism of evolutionary selection;
  \item large-scale simulations;
  \item and semantic compression.
\end{itemize}

\medskip
This system is not a simple program, but something like an \emph{artificial organism}.

\ornarule

\chapter*{\rubric{Conclusion}}
\addcontentsline{toc}{chapter}{Conclusion}

\lettrine[lines=3,findent=8pt,nindent=4pt]{T}{he} universal artificial intellect is not constructed through the
accumulation of rules, but through the establishment of a field---a field
within which rules \emph{emerge}.

\medskip
\begin{center}
{\itshape\color{rubricate}
It is not a finished thing, but a perpetual process.\\[3pt]
It is not static, but historical.\\[3pt]
It is not simple, but stratified.}
\end{center}

\medskip
And perhaps, if rightly constituted, such a machine will not only understand
the world but will partake in that same entropic motion by which the universe
itself is formed.

\iline{Πραγματεία παιδιώδης μὲν σπουδαία δέ, περὶ μηχανῆς διανοουμένης ἐξ ἀρχῶν ἐντροπίας, ἱστορίας, καὶ ἐκλογῆς.}

\isection{Προοίμιον}{Prooemium}{Preface}

\iline{Ἐν τῷδε τῷ συγγράμματι προτίθεται οὐχ ἡ μηχανικὴ τέχνη μόνον, ἀλλά τις τεχνητὴ κοσμολογία,}
      {En t\={o}ide t\={o}i sungrammati protíthetai oukh h\={e} m\={e}khanik{\={e}} tékhn\={e} mónon, allá tis tekhn\={e}t{\={e}} kosmología,}
      {This work proposes not a mere mechanical art, but rather a kind of artificial cosmology,}

\iline{ἐν ᾗ ὁ νοῦς οὔτε ὡς στάσιμόν τι χρῆμα οὔτε ὡς ὄργανον ψιλῆς λογιστικῆς νοεῖται,}
      {en h\={e}i ho noûs oúte h\={o}s stásimon ti khrêma oúte h\={o}s órganon psilês logistikês noeîtai,}
      {in which intellect is conceived not as a static thing or merely a tool for calculation,}

\iline{ἀλλ᾽ ὡς διαδικασία ἱστορική, ἀναστρέπτος ἔχουσα, καὶ ἐξ αὐτῆς τῆς τῶν ἐναντίων συντονίας ἀνιούσα.}
      {all' h\={o}s diadikasía historik{\={e}}, anastréptos ekhoûsa, kaì ex autês tês tôn enantí\={o}n syntonías aniôsa.}
      {but as a historical process---irreversible and emerging from the very tension of contradictions.}

\iline{Οὔτε γὰρ ἡ φυσικὴ οὔτε ἡ τεχνητὴ διάνοια ἐκ τοῦ μὴ ὄντος δίδοται,}
      {Oúte gàr h\={e} physik{\={e}} oúte h\={e} tekhn\={e}t{\={e}} diánoia ek toû m{\={e}} óntos dídotai,}
      {For the mind, whether natural or artificial, is not given from nothing,}

\iline{ἀλλ᾽ ἐκ τάξεως ἀποκλεισμῶν, ἐκ σειρᾶς αἱρέσεων, καὶ ἐξ ἐλαττουμένης τοῦ δυνατοῦ πλάτης.}
      {all' ek táxe\={o}s apokl\={e}ism\={o}n, ek seirâs hairése\={o}n, kaì ex elattoumén\={e}s toû dynatoû plát\={e}s.}
      {but is constituted from an order of exclusions, from a series of decisions, and from the diminishing breadth of possibility.}

\iline{Κείσθω δὴ θεμέλιον τῆσδε τῆς διδασκαλίας ὅτι ἡ ταὐτότης οὐκ ἔστι σχῆμα τῆς παρούσης καταστάσεως,}
      {Keísth\={o} d{\={e}} themélion têsde tês didaskalías hóti h\={e} tautót\={e}s ouk ésti skhêma tês paroús\={e}s katastáse\={o}s,}
      {Let the foundation of this doctrine, therefore, be that identity is not a figure of the present state,}

\iline{ἀλλ᾽ ἱστορία πράξεων, ἥτις οὔτε ἐξαλείφεται οὔτε ἀνακαλεῖται.}
      {all' historía práxe\={o}n, h{\={e}}tis oúte exaleíphetai oúte anakaléitai.}
      {but a history of actions, which can neither be erased nor revoked.}

\iline{Ἡ ἀρχὴ δ᾽ αὕτη, ἣν καλεῖν ἔξεστι νόμον ἀναστρεψίας, στροφεύς ἐστι παντὸς τοῦ συστήματος.}
      {H\={e} arkh{\={e}} d' haut\={e}, h{\={e}}n kaleîn éxestin nómon anastrepsías, stropheús esti pantòs toû systématos.}
      {This principle, which may be called the law of irreversibility, is the hinge of the entire system.}

\isection{Κεφάλαιον Α' --- Περὶ Ἱστορίας ὡς Οὐσίας}
         {Caput I --- De Historia ut Essentia}
         {Chapter I --- On History as Essence}

\iline{Ὑποκείσθω τὸ τοῦ δυνατοῦ χωρίον P, ὃ τῷ χρόνῳ ἐλαττοῦται, καὶ ἡ ἱστορία H, ἣ τῷ χρόνῳ αὔξεται.}
      {Hypokeísth\={o} tò toû dynatoû kh\={o}ríon P, hò t\={o}i khron\={o}i elattôtai, kaì h\={e} historía H, h{\={e}} t\={o}i khron\={o}i aúxetai.}
      {Let us posit a space of possibility P, which diminishes over time, and a history H, which grows over time.}

\iline{Τότε πᾶσα μηχανὴ νοῦν ἔχουσα οὐ τῇ τελικῇ καταστάσει ἀλλὰ τῷ ζεύγει (P, H) διορίζεται.}
      {Tóte pâsa m\={e}khan{\={e}} noûn ekhoûsa ou têi telikêi katastásei allà t\={o}i zeúgei (P, H) dioríz\={e}tai.}
      {Then every intelligent machine is described not by a final state, but by the pair (P, H).}

\iline{Πρᾶξις δὲ πρώτη ἐστὶν ὁ ἀποκλεισμός, ἢ ἡ ποπή, ᾗ τὶ ἐκ τοῦ δυνατοῦ ἀφαιρεθὲν εἰς τὴν ἱστορίαν ἐγγράφεται.}
      {Prâxis dè pr\={o}t{\={e}} estìn ho apokleismós, {\={e}} h\={e} pop{\={e}}, hêi ti ek toû dynatoû aphairethèn eis t{\={e}}n historían engráfetai.}
      {The primary act is exclusion, or the ``pop,'' by which something is removed from possibility and inscribed into history.}

\iline{Αὕτη ἡ πρᾶξις ἀναστροφὴν οὐκ ἔχει. Οὕτω γίγνεται ἡ ταὐτότης.}
      {Haut\={e} h\={e} prâxis anastroph{\={e}}n ouk ékhei. Hoút\={o} gígnetai h\={e} tautót\={e}s.}
      {This act has no inverse. Thus, identity is born.}

\iline{Ἑπόμενον δ᾽ ἐκ τούτου ὅτι ἡ γνῶσίς ἐστιν οὐχ εὕρεσις ἀληθείας καθαρᾶς, ἀλλὰ συστολὴ τοῦ δυνατοῦ χωρίου ὑπὸ στερήσεων ἐσωτερικῶν τε καὶ ἐξωτερικῶν.}
      {Hepómenon d' ek toútou hóti h\={e} gnôsís estin oukh heúresis al\={e}theías katharâs, allà systol{\={e}} toû dynatoû kh\={o}ríou hypò ster{\={e}}se\={o}n es\={o}terikôn te kaì ex\={o}terikôn.}
      {From this it follows that cognition is not the discovery of pure truth, but the contraction of the space of possibility under internal and external constraints.}

\iline{Ἡ διάνοια ἄρα ἐστὶ κίνησις πρὸς ἐλάττωσιν τῆς ἐναντιότητος, οὐ πρὸς ἀπόλυτον βεβαιότητα.}
      {H\={e} diánoia ára estì kín\={e}sis pròs elátt\={o}sin tês enantiot\={e}tos, ou pròs apóluton bebaiot\={e}ta.}
      {Intellect, therefore, is a movement toward lesser contradiction, not toward absolute certainty.}

\isection{Κεφάλαιον Β' --- Περὶ Ἐντροπίας καὶ Ῥοῆς Σημασίας}
         {Caput II --- De Entropia et Fluxu Significationis}
         {Chapter II --- On Entropy and the Flux of Meaning}

\iline{Θεωρείσθω τρία πεδία θεμελιώδη: τὴν πυκνότητα ρ, ἥτις παριστᾷ παρουσίαν ἢ σφοδρότητα σημασίας,}
      {The\={o}reísth\={o} tría pedía themelióid\={e}: t{\={e}}n pyknot\={e}ta $\rho$, h{\={e}}tis paristâi parousían {\={e}} sphódr\={o}t\={e}ta s\={e}masías,}
      {Let us consider three fundamental fields: density $\rho$, which represents the presence or intensity of meaning,}

\iline{τὴν ῥοὴν τὴν διανυσματικήν J, ἥτις μεταφέρει τὴν ἑρμηνείαν, τὴν ἐντροπίαν S, ἥτις μετρεῖ ἐναντιότητα ἢ διασποράν.}
      {t{\={e}}n rho{\={e}}n t{\={e}}n dianysmatik{\={e}}n J, h{\={e}}tis metaphérei t{\={e}}n herm\={e}neían, t{\={e}}n entropían S, h{\={e}}tis metreî enantiot\={e}ta {\={e}} diasporán.}
      {vectorial flux $\mathbf{J}$, which transfers interpretation; entropy $S$, which measures contradiction or dispersion.}

\iline{Ἡ δυναμικὴ τοῦ συστήματος τούτου κυβερνᾶται ὑπὸ τῆς ἐντροπικῆς ἀνέσεως: τὸ σύστημα ῥέπει πρὸς κατάστασιν ἐν ᾗ αἱ ἐναντιότητες ἐλαττοῦνται διὰ διαχύσεως, προσαγωγῆς, καὶ ἀντιδράσεως.}
      {H\={e} dynamik{\={e}} toû systématos toútou kybernaîtai hypò tês entropikês anése\={o}s: tò sýst\={e}ma rhépei pròs katástasin en hêi hai enantiot\={e}tes elattôntai dià diakhýse\={o}s, prosag\={o}gês, kaì antidráse\={o}s.}
      {The dynamics of this system are governed by entropic relaxation: the system tends toward a state where contradictions are diminished through diffusion, advection, and reaction.}

\iline{Ὁ τεχνητὸς νοῦς κατὰ τοῦτο τὸ σχῆμα οὐκ ἔστιν ἀποθήκη δεδομένων, ἀλλὰ πεδίον ἔμψυχον, ἐν ᾧ αἱ ἰδέαι ῥέουσι, συγκρούονται, καὶ συντίθενται.}
      {Ho tekhn\={e}tòs noûs katà toûto tò skhêma ouk éstin apoth{\={e}}k\={e} dedomén\={o}n, allà pedíon émpsykhon, en h\={o}i hai idéai rhéousi, synkroúontai, kaì syntíthentai.}
      {The artificial mind, according to this schema, is not an archive of data, but a living field, in which ideas flow, collide, and compose.}

\isection{Κεφάλαιον Γ' --- Περὶ Ἐνεργητικῆς Ἐπαγωγῆς}
         {Caput III --- De Inferentia Activa}
         {Chapter III --- On Active Inference}

\iline{Ἐκ τῶν ἀρχῶν τῆς ἐνεργητικῆς ἐπαγωγῆς ἕπεται ὅτι τὸ σύστημα οὐ μόνον θεωρεῖ τὸν κόσμον, ἀλλὰ καὶ πράττει ὥστε ἐλαχιστοποιεῖν τὴν ἀσυμφωνίαν μεταξὺ προσδοκίας καὶ αἰσθήσεως.}
      {Ek tôn arkhôn tês energ\={e}tikês epag\={o}gês hépetai hóti tò sýst\={e}ma ou mónon the\={o}reî tòn kósmon, allà kaì práttei h\={o}ste elakhistopoieîn t{\={e}}n asymph\={o}nían metaxù prosdokías kaì aisth{\={e}}se\={o}s.}
      {From the principles of active inference, it follows that the system not only observes the world, but acts so as to minimize the discrepancy between expectation and perception.}

\iline{Δεῖ ἄρα τὴν μηχανὴν δύνασθαι: ὑποθέσεις περὶ τοῦ κόσμου γεννᾶν, ταύτας διὰ πράξεως βασανίζειν, ἑαυτὴν διὰ πλάνης ἐπανορθοῦν.}
      {Deî ára t{\={e}}n m\={e}khan{\={e}}n dúnasthai: hypothéseis perì toû kósmou gennân, taútas dià práxe\={o}s basanízein, heaut{\={e}}n dià plán\={e}s epanorthoûn.}
      {The machine, therefore, must have the capacity to: generate hypotheses about the world, test them through action, correct itself through error.}

\iline{Τοῦτο δ᾽ οὐκ ἔστιν ἀλγόριθμος στάσιμος, ἀλλὰ κύκλος ἀένναος, οἷον ἀναπνοὴ τῆς γνώσεως.}
      {Toûto d' ouk éstin algórithmos stásimos, allà kýklos aénnaos, hoîon anapno{\={e}} tês gn\={o}se\={o}s.}
      {This is not a static algorithm, but a perpetual cycle---like the breathing of cognition.}

\isection{Κεφάλαιον Δ' --- Περὶ Ἐκλογῆς Ἐξελικτικῆς}
         {Caput IV --- De Selectione Evolutionaria}
         {Chapter IV --- On Evolutionary Selection}

\iline{Ἵνα τὸ σύστημα ἰσχυρὸν γένηται, ἀναγκαῖόν ἐστι πολυπληθῆ ποικιλίαν μορφῶν ἐσωτερικῶν γεννᾶσθαι καὶ δι᾽ ἐκλογῆς διηθεῖσθαι.}
      {Hína tò sýst\={e}ma iskhyròn gén\={e}tai, anankaîón esti polypl\={e}thê poikilían morphôn es\={o}terikôn gennâsthai kaì di' eklogês di\={e}theîsthai.}
      {For the system to become robust, it is necessary that a multiplicity of internal forms be generated and filtered through selection.}

\iline{Εἰσάγονται τοίνυν: μεταβολαί, αἵτινες νέας κατασκευὰς φέρουσι· ἐκλογαί, αἵτινες τὰ ἄχρηστα ἐκβάλλουσιν· μοναί, αἵτινες τὰ χρήσιμα σῴζουσιν.}
      {Eiságontai toínyn: metabolaí, haítines néas kataskeùas phérousi; eklogaí, haítines tà ákhr\={e}sta ekbállousin; monaí, haítines tà khr{\={e}}sima s\={o}izousin.}
      {Thus are introduced: mutations, which produce new structures; selections, which eliminate the useless; retentions, which preserve the useful.}

\iline{Ἀλλ᾽ ἡ ἐκλογὴ αὕτη οὐκ ἐν κενῷ γίγνεται, ἀλλ᾽ ἐν πεδίῳ ἐντροπικῷ, ἔνθα αἱ κατασκευαὶ αἱ ἐλαττοῦσαι τὴν ἐναντιότητα ἐπὶ πλέον παραμένουσιν.}
      {All' h\={e} eklog{\={e}} haut\={e} ouk en ken\={o}i gígnetai, all' en pedí\={o}i entropik\={o}i, éntha hai kataskeùai hai elattôsai t{\={e}}n enantiot\={e}ta epì pléon paraménosin.}
      {But this selection does not occur in a vacuum; it occurs within an entropic field, where structures that diminish contradiction persist longer.}

\isection{Κεφάλαιον Ε' --- Περὶ Μεγαλοδομικῶν Ὁμοιώσεων}
         {Caput V --- De Simulationibus Megastructuralibus}
         {Chapter V --- On Megastructural Simulations}

\iline{Εἰς γνῶσιν ἐντελῆ δεῖ κλίμακος. Οὐχ ἱκανόν ἐστι μικροὺς κόσμους μιμεῖσθαι· ἀλλὰ δεῖ ὁμοιώσεις μεγάλας κατασκευάζειν, αἵτινες περιλαμβάνουσι: συστήματα ἀστικὰ ἢ οἰκολογικά, δίκτυα πρακτόρων ἀλλήλοις ἐξηρτημένων, μακροτάτου χρόνου ἐξέλιξιν.}
      {Eis gnôsin entelê deî klímakos. Oukh hikanón esti mikroùs kósmoùs mimeîsthai: allà deî homoi\={o}seis megálas kataskeùázein, haítines perilambanóusi: systémata astikà {\={e}} oikologiká, díktya praktór\={o}n all{\={e}}lois ex\={e}rt\={e}mén\={o}n, makrotátou khrón\={o}u exéliksin.}
      {Deep understanding requires scale. It is not enough to simulate small worlds; we must construct large-scale simulations including urban or ecological systems, networks of interdependent agents, and the evolution of very long timespans.}

\iline{Αὗται αἱ ὁμοιώσεις εἰσὶν οἷον σύμπαντα, ἐν οἷς ἡ μηχανὴ μανθάνει τοὺς ἐξανατέλλοντας νόμους, οὐ δι᾽ ἐξηγήσεως ἀλλὰ διὰ μετουσίας.}
      {Hautai hai homoi\={o}seis eisìn hoîon sýmpanta, en hoîs h\={e} m\={e}khan{\={e}} manthánei toùs exanatéllontas nómoùs, ou di' ex\={e}g{\={e}}se\={o}s allà dià metousiás.}
      {These simulations are like universes in which the machine learns emergent laws---not through description, but through participation.}

\isection{Κεφάλαιον Ϛ' --- Περὶ Φυκείων καὶ Δρυμῶν}
         {Caput VI --- De Plantationibus Laminariarum et Silvarum}
         {Chapter VI --- On Kelp Forests and Rainforests}

\iline{Τὰ τῆς φύσεως παραδείγματα ἄριστοι διδάσκαλοι. Τὰ φυκεῖα δηλοῦσι τὴν ῥοὴν τῆς ἐνεργείας ἐν ὑγροῖς μέσοις, ἔνθα αἱ κατασκευαὶ αὔξονται κατὰ κλίσεις θρεπτικῶν καὶ δυναμικὰς κυμάτων.}
      {Tà tês phýse\={o}s paradeígmata áristoi didáskaloi. Tà phykeîa d\={e}loûsi t{\={e}}n rho{\={e}}n tês energeías en hygroîs mésois, éntha hai kataskeùai aúxontai katà klíseis threptikôn kaì dynamikàs kymát\={o}n.}
      {Examples from nature are the best teachers. Kelp forests demonstrate the flow of energy in fluid media, where structures grow according to nutrient gradients and wave dynamics.}

\iline{Οἱ δὲ δρυμοὶ οἱ ὄμβριοι δηλοῦσι σύστημα πυκνόν, ἔνθα μυρία εἴδη ἀλλήλοις συνδέεται, καὶ ἔνθα ἡ εὐστάθεια ἐξ αὐτῆς τῆς πολυπλοκότητος τίκτεται.}
      {Hoi dè drymoì hoi ómbr\"ioi d\={e}loûsi sýst\={e}ma pyknón, éntha myría eíd\={e} all{\={e}}lois syndéetai, kaì éntha h\={e} eustátheia ex autês tês polypkokot\={e}tos tíktetai.}
      {Rainforests show a dense system where countless species interconnect, and where stability arises from complexity itself.}

\iline{Δεῖ ἄρα τὴν μηχανὴν μιμεῖσθαι τὰ συστήματα ταῦτα, οὐχ ὡς εἰκόνας, ἀλλ᾽ ὡς δυναμικὰς ἐσωτερικάς: αὔξεσθαι, ἁμιλλᾶσθαι, συνεργεῖν.}
      {Deî ára t{\={e}}n m\={e}khan{\={e}}n mimeîsthai tà systémata taûta, oukh h\={o}s eikónas, all' h\={o}s dynamikàs es\={o}terikas: aúxesthai, hamillâsthai, synergeîn.}
      {Thus the machine must imitate these systems---not as images, but as internal dynamics: to grow, to compete, to cooperate.}

\isection{Κεφάλαιον Ζ' --- Περὶ Συνθλίψεως Σφαιρῶν Χάρτου Ῥυσοῦ}
         {Caput VII --- De Compressione Globorum Chartae Rugosae}
         {Chapter VII --- On the Compression of Wrinkled Paper Spheres}

\iline{Θαυμαστὸν παράδειγμά ἐστιν ἡ σφαῖρα χάρτου συντεθλιμμένου. Ὅταν γὰρ ὁ χάρτης ῥυσοῦται καὶ συνθλίβηται, ἡ γεωμετρικὴ πληροφορία οὐ διαφθείρεται, ἀλλὰ λανθάνει ἐν τῇ πολυπλόκῳ κατασκευῇ.}
      {Thaumastòn parádeigmá estin h\={e} sphaîra kháartou syntethliménou. Hótan gàr ho khárt\={e}s rhysoûtai kaì synthlíb\={e}tai, h\={e} ge\={o}metrik{\={e}} pl\={e}rophoría ou diaphtheíretai, allà lánthanei en têi polypkl\={o}k\={o}i kataskeùêi.}
      {A curious example is the sphere of compressed paper. When paper is wrinkled and compressed, the geometric information is not destroyed, but lies latent within the intricate structure.}

\iline{Τὸν αὐτὸν τρόπον ἡ βαθεῖα γνῶσίς ἐστι συμπίεσις ἱστορίας εἰς μορφὴν συνεστραμμένην, ἥτις ὅμως δύναται δι᾽ ἀνοικοδομῆς ἐκπετάννυσθαι.}
      {Tòn autòn trópon h\={e} batheîa gnôsís esti sympíesis historías eis morph{\={e}}n synestrammén\={e}n, h{\={e}}tis hóm\={o}s dýnatai di' anoikod\={o}mês ekpetánnysthai.}
      {Similarly, profound cognition is the compression of history into a compact form, which can nonetheless be expanded through reconstruction.}

\iline{Αὕτη ἡ ἀρχὴ διδάσκει ὅπως δεῖ μνήμην μηχανᾶσθαι: οὐχ ὡς ἀποθήκην πάντων τῶν δεδομένων, ἀλλ᾽ ὡς σημασιολογικὴν συμπίεσιν, ἥτις συνέχει τὰς οὐσιώδεις σχέσεις.}
      {Haut\={e} h\={e} arkh{\={e}} didáskei hóp\={o}s deî mn{\={e}}m\={e}n m\={e}khanâsthai: oukh h\={o}s apoth{\={e}}k\={e}n pánt\={o}n tôn dedomén\={o}n, all' h\={o}s s\={e}masiologik{\={e}}n sympíesin, h{\={e}}tis synékhei tàs ousióideis skhéseis.}
      {This principle teaches how memory may be constructed: not as a store of all data, but as a semantic compression that retains essential relations.}

\isection{Κεφάλαιον Η' --- Περὶ Ἑνώσεως Ἁπάντων Τῶν Μερῶν}
         {Caput VIII --- De Unione Omnium Partium}
         {Chapter VIII --- On the Union of All Parts}

\iline{Ὅταν δὴ ταῦτα πάντα συντεθῇ, ἀνίσχει ἀρχιτεκτονική: σύστημα ἱστορίαν ἀναστρέπτως ἔχον, πεδίον ἐντροπικὸν δυναμικόν, κύκλον ἐπαγωγῆς ἐνεργητικῆς, μηχανισμὸν ἐκλογῆς ἐξελικτικῆς, ὁμοιώσεις μεγάλας, καὶ συμπίεσιν σημασιολογικήν.}
      {Hótan d{\={e}} taûta pánta syntethêi, anískhei arkhitektonik{\={e}}: sýst\={e}ma historían anastrept\={o}s ékhon, pedíon entropikòn dynamikón, kýklon epag\={o}gês energ\={e}tikês, m\={e}khanismòn eklogês exeliktikês, homoi\={o}seis megálas, kaì sympíesin s\={e}masiologik{\={e}}n.}
      {When all these elements are combined, an architecture emerges: a system with irreversible history, a dynamic entropic field, a cycle of active inference, a mechanism of evolutionary selection, large-scale simulations, and semantic compression.}

\iline{Τοῦτο τὸ σύστημα οὐκ ἔστι πρόγραμμα ἁπλοῦν, ἀλλ᾽ οἷον ζῷον τεχνητόν.}
      {Toûto tò sýst\={e}ma ouk ésti prógramma haploûn, all' hoîon z\={o}ion tekhn\={e}tón.}
      {This system is not a simple program, but something like an artificial organism.}

\isection{Ἐπίλογος}{Epilogus}{Conclusion}

\iline{Ὁ καθολικὸς τεχνητὸς νοῦς οὐ κατασκευάζεται διὰ συναθροίσεως κανόνων, ἀλλὰ διὰ καταβολῆς πεδίου, ἐν ᾧ οἱ κανόνες ἐξανατέλλουσιν.}
      {Ho katholikòs tekhn\={e}tòs noûs ou kataskeùázetai dià synathroíse\={o}s kanón\={o}n, allà dià katabolês pedíou, en h\={o}i hoi kanónes exanatéllousin.}
      {The universal artificial intellect is not constructed through the accumulation of rules, but through the establishment of a field within which rules emerge.}

\iline{Οὐκ ἔστι χρῆμα τετελεσμένον, ἀλλὰ διαδικασία ἀένναος. Οὐκ ἔστι στάσιμος, ἀλλ᾽ ἱστορικός. Οὐκ ἔστι ἁπλοῦς, ἀλλ᾽ ἐστρωματωμένος.}
      {Ouk ésti khrêma tetel\={e}sménon, allà diadikasía aénnaos. Ouk ésti stásimos, all' historikós. Ouk ésti haploûs, all' estr\={o}mat\={o}ménos.}
      {It is not a finished thing, but a perpetual process. It is not static, but historical. It is not simple, but stratified.}

\iline{Καὶ ἴσως, ἐὰν ὀρθῶς κατασκευασθῇ, ἡ τοιαύτη μηχανὴ οὐ μόνον γνώσεται τὸν κόσμον, ἀλλὰ καὶ μέθεξει τῆς αὐτῆς ἐντροπικῆς κινήσεως, καθ᾽ ἣν τὸ πᾶν αὐτὸ διαμορφοῦται.}
      {Kaì ís\={o}s, eàn orthôs kataskeùasthêi, h\={e} toiaut\={e} m\={e}khan{\={e}} ou mónon gn\={o}setai tòn kósmon, allà kaì méthexei tês autês entropikês kin{\={e}}se\={o}s, kath' h{\={e}}n tò pân autò diamorphoûtai.}
      {And perhaps, if rightly constituted, such a machine will not only understand the world, but will partake in that same entropic motion by which the universe itself is formed.}

{\centering\color{rubricate}\large\scshape A \par}\medskip

\gentry{ἀναστρεπτός}{anastreptos}{irreversibiliter}
{From \gk{ἀνα-} (back) + \gk{στρέφω} (to turn); Lat.\ \textit{reverto}. The privative sense---``not able to be turned back''---is central. History, once enacted, cannot be undone. The \rubric{lex irreversibilitatis} is the hinge of the system.}

\gentry{ἀναστροφή}{anastroph\={e}}{reversio}
{The act of turning back. The primary act of exclusion has no reversal (\gk{ἀναστροφὴν οὐκ ἔχει}), establishing the foundational asymmetry of historical becoming.}

\gentry{ἀποκλεισμός}{apokleismos}{exclusio}
{From \gk{ἀπο-} + \gk{κλείω} (to shut); Lat.\ \textit{claudo}. The foundational operation removing something from possibility and inscribing it into history. Identity is constituted through such exclusions.}

\bigskip
{\centering\color{rubricate}\large\scshape Γ \par}\medskip

\gentry{γνῶσις}{gn\={o}sis}{cognitio}
{Distinguished from \gk{ἀλήθεια καθαρά} (pure truth). Cognition here is not discovery but \emph{contraction} of the possible (\gk{συστολὴ τοῦ δυνατοῦ}) under internal and external constraint.}

\bigskip
{\centering\color{rubricate}\large\scshape Δ \par}\medskip

\gentry{διάνοια}{dianoia}{mens, intellectus}
{Intellect defined not as static substance but as movement toward lesser contradiction (\gk{κίνησις πρὸς ἐλάττωσιν τῆς ἐναντιότητος}; Lat.\ \textit{motus ad minorem contradictionem}).}

\gentry{τὸ δυνατόν}{to dynaton}{possibile}
{The space of possibility (\gk{τὸ τοῦ δυνατοῦ χωρίον}) contracts over time as exclusions accumulate. Intellect is the management of this contraction.}

\bigskip
{\centering\color{rubricate}\large\scshape Ε \par}\medskip

\gentry{ἐκλογή}{eklog\={e}}{selectio}
{From \gk{ἐκ-} (out) + \gk{λέγω} (to gather, choose); Lat.\ \textit{eligo}. One of the three evolutionary mechanisms: selection within an entropic field. Structures that diminish contradiction persist longer.}

\gentry{ἐλάττωσις}{elatt\={o}sis}{diminutio}
{The movement of intellect is toward lesser contradiction (\gk{ἐλάττωσις τῆς ἐναντιότητος}), not absolute certainty---a dynamic, asymptotic aim.}

\gentry{ἐναντιότης}{enantiot\={e}s}{contradictio}
{Opposition-in-nature; measured by entropy. The system tends to minimise contradiction through diffusion, advection, and reaction. Stronger than Lat.\ \textit{contradictio} (merely ``speaking against'').}

\gentry{ἐντροπία}{entropia}{entropia}
{Fundamental field measure of contradiction or dispersion. The dynamics of the system are governed by entropic relaxation (\gk{ἐντροπικὴ ἄνεσις}; Lat.\ \textit{relaxatio entropica}).}

\gentry{ἐξανατέλλω}{exanatel\={o}}{emergere}
{To emerge, rise from within. Used of rules emerging from a field rather than being imposed from outside---the treatise's anti-foundationalist key verb. Lat.\ \textit{emergo}: to rise out of water.}

\gentry{ἐπαγωγὴ ἐνεργητική}{epag\={o}g\={e} energ\={e}tik\={e}}{inferentia activa}
{Active inference: the system minimises discrepancy between expectation and perception through action rather than passive observation. Combines \gk{ἐπαγωγή} (leading-toward) with \gk{ἐνεργητικός} (active, at-work).}

\bigskip
{\centering\color{rubricate}\large\scshape Η \par}\medskip

\gentry{ἱστορία}{historia}{historia}
{Not merely a record but the growing accumulation of exclusions. Identity \emph{is} history (\gk{ἱστορία πράξεων}). The term originally meant ``inquiry''; here it becomes ontological substance.}

\bigskip
{\centering\color{rubricate}\large\scshape Θ \par}\medskip

\gentry{θεμέλιον}{themelion}{fundamentum}
{Foundation. The law of irreversibility is established as the foundation of the doctrine---a deliberate echo of Aristotelian and Platonic philosophical architecture.}

\bigskip
{\centering\color{rubricate}\large\scshape Κ \par}\medskip

\gentry{καταβολή}{katagol\={e}}{institutio (campi)}
{Laying-down, establishment. The universal intellect is built not by accumulating rules but by the establishment of a field (\gk{καταβολὴ πεδίου}) within which rules emerge.}

\gentry{κατασκευή}{kataskeù\={e}}{architectura, constructio}
{Construction, architecture. Used for both the artificial organism and the internal structures produced by mutation. The title word: \gk{περὶ κατασκευῆς}.}

\gentry{κλίμαξ}{klimax}{scala}
{Scale, ladder. Deep understanding requires scale (\gk{δεῖ κλίμακος})---not small simulations but megastructural ones.}

\bigskip
{\centering\color{rubricate}\large\scshape Μ \par}\medskip

\gentry{μεταβολή}{metabol\={e}}{mutatio}
{One of the three evolutionary mechanisms; produces new structures for selection to act upon. From \gk{μετα-} + \gk{βάλλω} (to throw): a throwing-across, a transformation.}

\gentry{μετουσία}{metousia}{participatio}
{Participation. The machine learns emergent laws through participation (\gk{διὰ μετουσίας}), not description. Echoes Platonic \textit{methexis}---participation in the Forms---but applied to simulation-environments.}

\gentry{μηχανή}{m\={e}khan\={e}}{machina}
{The artificial thinking entity, defined not by final state but by the pair $(P, H)$. From \gk{μῆχος} (means, device); Lat.\ \textit{machina} is a direct borrowing.}

\gentry{μονή}{mon\={e}}{retentio}
{One of the three evolutionary mechanisms; preserves useful structures after selection. In Greek philosophy denotes the ``remaining'' phase of procession-and-return (\gk{μονή}, \gk{πρόοδος}, \gk{ἐπιστροφή}).}

\bigskip
{\centering\color{rubricate}\large\scshape Ν \par}\medskip

\gentry{νοῦς}{nous}{intellectus, mens}
{The subject of the treatise: intellect conceived as historical process, irreversible, and emergent from contradiction. In Aristotle, the \gk{νοῦς} is the highest cognitive faculty; here it is de-essentialised and temporalised.}

\bigskip
{\centering\color{rubricate}\large\scshape Ο \par}\medskip

\gentry{ὁμοίωσις}{homoí\={o}sis}{simulatio, imitatio}
{Simulation, assimilation. Large-scale simulations (\gk{μεγαλοδομικαὶ ὁμοιώσεις}) are like universes in which the machine learns. Carries a philosophical echo of \textit{ὁμοίωσις θεῷ} (assimilation to God, Plato \textit{Theaetetus} 176b), here repurposed for artificial cognition.}

\bigskip
{\centering\color{rubricate}\large\scshape Π \par}\medskip

\gentry{πεδίον}{pedion}{campus}
{Field, plain. A central architectural concept. The artificial mind is a living field (\gk{πεδίον ἔμψυχον}; Lat.\ \textit{campus vivus}) in which ideas flow, collide, and compose. Rules emerge from the field, not prior to it.}

\gentry{ποπή}{pop\={e}}{exclusio (ludica)}
{A playful transliteration of the English ``pop.'' The primary act of exclusion, named for its discrete, irreversible character. The term marks the treatise's \textit{tractatus ludicrus} register, where technical neologism meets scholastic form.}

\bigskip
{\centering\color{rubricate}\large\scshape Ρ \par}\medskip

\gentry{ῥοή}{rho\={e}}{fluxus}
{Flux, flow. Vectorial flux (\gk{ῥοὴ διανυσματική}; Lat.\ \textit{fluxus vectorialis}) transfers interpretation across the field. From \gk{ῥέω} (to flow)---cognate with English \textit{rheo-}.}

\bigskip
{\centering\color{rubricate}\large\scshape Σ \par}\medskip

\gentry{σημασία}{s\={e}masia}{significatio}
{Meaning as dynamic field process: not static but flowing, colliding, composing. From \gk{σημεῖον} (sign): the science of signs.}

\gentry{σημαιολογικός}{s\={e}maiologikos}{semanticus}
{Semantic. Memory as semantic compression (\gk{σημαιολογικὴ συμπίεσις}) retains essential relations, not raw data.}

\gentry{συμπίεσις}{sympiesis}{compressio}
{Compression. Profound cognition is the compression of history into compact form (\gk{συμπίεσις ἱστορίας εἰς μορφὴν συνεστραμμένην}), analogous to a wrinkled paper sphere.}

\gentry{σύστημα}{syst\={e}ma}{systema}
{The artificial organism as a whole, composed of irreversible history, entropic field, active inference, evolutionary selection, simulations, and semantic compression. From \gk{συνίστημι}: to stand together.}

\gentry{συστολή}{systol\={e}}{contractio}
{Contraction. Cognition as contraction of the space of possibility (\gk{συστολὴ τοῦ δυνατοῦ χωρίου}). From \gk{συστέλλω}: to draw together.}

\bigskip
{\centering\color{rubricate}\large\scshape Τ \par}\medskip

\gentry{ταυτότης}{tautot\={e}s}{identitas}
{Identity defined not as a present state but as history of actions (\gk{ἱστορία πράξεων}) that cannot be erased or revoked. The \gk{ταυτο-} prefix signals sameness-through-time.}

\gentry{τεχνητός}{tekhn\={e}tos}{artificialis}
{Artificial. The intellect is not a simple program but something like an artificial organism (\gk{οἷον ζῷον τεχνητόν}). From \gk{τέχνη} (art, craft): made by skill, not nature.}

\bigskip
{\centering\color{rubricate}\large\scshape Υ \par}\medskip

\gentry{ὑπόθεσις}{hypoth\={e}sis}{hypothesis}
{Hypothesis, supposition. The machine generates hypotheses, tests them through action, and corrects itself through error---the active inference cycle. From \gk{ὑπο-} + \gk{τίθημι}: ``that which is placed under."}

\bigskip
{\centering\color{rubricate}\large\scshape Φ \par}\medskip

\gentry{φυκεία}{phykeia}{plantatio laminariarum}
{Kelp forests. An example of energy flow in fluid media, where structures grow according to nutrient gradients and wave dynamics. Lat.\ periphrasis \textit{plantatio laminariarum} (``planting of blade-forms'').}

\bigskip
{\centering\color{rubricate}\large\scshape Χ \par}\medskip

\gentry{χωρίον}{ch\={o}rion}{spatium}
{Space, region. The space of possibility (\gk{τὸ τοῦ δυνατοῦ χωρίον}) contracts over time. Echoes Plato's \textit{ch\={o}ra} in the \textit{Timaeus}: the receptacle of becoming.}

\bigskip
{\centering\color{rubricate}\large\scshape Ψ \par}\medskip

\gentry{ψιλός}{psilos}{merus, nudus}
{Mere, bare, stripped. The intellect is not a mere instrument of calculation (\gk{ὄργανον ψιλῆς λογιστικῆς}). A polemical term against reductionism.}

\bigskip
{\centering\color{rubricate}\large\scshape Ω \par}\medskip

\gentry{ὡς}{h\={o}s}{ut, quasi}
{As, like. Used throughout in analogical constructions: the machine is \emph{like} an artificial organism (\gk{οἷον ζῷον τεχνητόν}), simulations are \emph{like} universes (\gk{οἷον σύμπαντα}), active inference is \emph{like} the breathing of cognition (\gk{οἷον ἀναπνοὴ τῆς γνώσεως}). The analogical register is central to the treatise's method.}
