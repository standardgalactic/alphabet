- **Tracks text, not truth**

Can be modeled as a semantic field failure where:

```

∇S · v > 0   (user effort increases entropy)
```

- Trained via **SGD**, producing **fractured, entangled representations**
- Learns *correlations*, not *factorized structure*
- Example: model learns to count chickens and pencils separately but does not form a reusable, modular concept of *counting*

**Common failure:**  
> Information is optimized for retrieval and engagement, not semantic compositional truth.
