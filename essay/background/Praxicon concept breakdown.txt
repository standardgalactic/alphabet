AI Overview
"Praxicon" can refer to a cognitive science concept of a conceptual knowledge base linking language and sensorimotor representations, a theoretical model for therapeutic intervention in developmental disabilities called the PRAXICON, and the specific computational knowledge resource known as the PRAXICON based on this model. It also refers to several unrelated companies, including Praxicon LLC, a project management consulting firm, and Praxicon Events, a company that uses cookies for website usability. 
In Cognitive Science and AI
Conceptual Knowledge Base:
The term "praxicon" (or PRAXICON) is used to describe a grounding resource that couples natural language with sensorimotor representations. It serves as a semantic network of multi-representational concepts, facilitating the integration of multimodal and multimedia content. 
Grounding Resource:
The PRAXICON is a computational resource and database that integrates information from various modules (e.g., language, vision, motor) to ground knowledge. 
Intelligent Systems:
The concept of a PRAXICON is considered necessary for intelligent systems to achieve large-scale generalization and common sense reasoning beyond "one-shot learning". 
In Therapy and Research 
Therapeutic Model:
The PRAXICON is a structured intervention model for developmental disabilities, based on Roy's conceptual-productive model of praxis.
Integration of Theories:
This therapeutic model integrates developmental theories from Vygotsky, Piaget, and Thelen to target perceptual, conceptual, and motor systems.
Action Planning and Understanding:
The PRAXICON aims to improve action planning, symbolic understanding, and functional participation in everyday tasks by linking therapeutic activities to underlying conceptual and motor systems.
In Business
Praxicon LLC: A project management consulting firm based in Plano, Texas, specializing in Enterprise PMO, Project Portfolio Management, and Smartsheet applications. 
Praxicon Events: A company that uses cookies to improve the usability of its websites, but does not use them to gather unnecessary personal information. 
Praxicon GmbH: A company registered in Germany.
