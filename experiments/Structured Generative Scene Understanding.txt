Abstract
Modern generative models excel at producing visually compelling scenes but often fail
to capture physically plausible dynamics or semantically coherent structures. We introduce
TARTAN (Trajectory-Aware Recursive Tiling with Annotated Noise), a novel framework
that integrates recursive spatial tiling, Gaussian aura fields, and annotated noise to enforce
physical consistency and semantic modularity. Grounded in embodied oscillation dynamics
and categorical scene semantics, TARTAN leverages functorial mappings (S : O →CType)
and natural transformations to align symbolic and physical representations. By encoding
agent trajectories, biomechanical hierarchies, and memetic proxies, TARTAN achieves in-
terpretable, generalizable scene reconstruction. Empirical validations on synthetic and real-
world datasets demonstrate superior trajectory fidelity, entropy preservation, and semantic
alignment compared to emergent models like FER. TARTAN's layered design offers a princi-
pled alternative to black-box generative systems, enabling applications in high-fidelity video
synthesis, embodied robotics, and cognitive modeling. This work paves the way for genera-
tive architectures that reason like physicists and dream like poets.

Introduction
Deep generative models, such as NeRF (?) and VideoGPT (?), have revolutionized scene synthe-
sis but struggle with physical plausibility and semantic consistency across scales. These models
often rely on emergent representations that lack explicit mechanisms for encoding dynamics or
grounding symbolic meaning, leading to artifacts in complex scenes or failure under distribu-
tion shifts. For example, a generated video of a car chase may look cinematic but violate basic
physics, like gravity or momentum, rendering it useless for embodied applications like robotics
or AR/VR.
The TARTAN framework (Trajectory-Aware Recursive Tiling with Annotated Noise) ad-
dresses these limitations by integrating three core components: (1) a recursive tiling module
for multiscale spatial decomposition, (2) Gaussian aura fields encoding physical and intentional
dynamics, and (3) an annotated noise layer for semantic metadata embedding. Drawing on em-
bodied oscillation dynamics (?) and categorical scene semantics (?), TARTAN constructs scenes
as fibered categories with functorial mappings (S : O →CType) and natural transformations
(η : S ⇒F), ensuring alignment between physical and symbolic layers.
Our contributions are threefold:
• TARTAN Framework: A novel architecture combining recursive tiling, Gaussian fields,
and semantic functor alignment for structured scene generation.
• Physical-Semantic Representation: Aura embeddings that capture velocity, intent, and
affective states, grounded in biomechanical hierarchies.
• Generative Blueprint: A reversible decoding mechanism leveraging oscillatory dynamics
for interpretable, physics-consistent reconstruction.
This framework not only outperforms emergent models like FER (?) in trajectory fidelity
and semantic alignment but also offers a modular, interpretable alternative for applications in
cognitive modeling, robotics, and high-fidelity video synthesis.
