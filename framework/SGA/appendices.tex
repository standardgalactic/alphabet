\section{PDEs and Boundary Conditions for RSVP Fields}
The RSVP framework introduces three coupled fields: scalar capacity $\Phi$, vector flow $v$, and entropy $S$. Their joint evolution can be expressed via gradient-flow equations derived from the RSVP action functional:
\begin{align}
\partial_t \Phi &= -\gamma_\Phi \frac{\delta F}{\delta \Phi} + f_\Phi, \\
\partial_t v    &= -\gamma_v \frac{\delta F}{\delta v} + f_v, \\
\partial_t S    &= -\gamma_S \frac{\delta F}{\delta S} + f_S,
\end{align}
where $F[\Phi,v,S]$ is the free-energy–like functional defined in the main text.  
Boundary conditions include:
\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Periodic:} $(\Phi,v,S)(x+L)=(\Phi,v,S)(x)$, appropriate for cosmological tilings.
    \item \textbf{Reflective:} $n\cdot v=0$ at $\partial \Omega$, for cognitive domains bounded by cortical geometry.
    \item \textbf{Absorbing:} $S\to S_0$ at infinity, used in entropy-dissipative cosmological models.
\end{itemize}
Well-posedness can be studied using methods from synergetics \cite{Haken1983} and dissipative systems \cite{Prigogine1980}.

\section{BV/AKSZ Technical Details}
The Batalin–Vilkovisky (BV) formalism \cite{BatalinVilkovisky1981} provides a natural setting for RSVP. The master equation
\[
(S_{\mathrm{BV}},S_{\mathrm{BV}})=0
\]
encodes consistency of the field algebra, while the Alexandrov-Kontsevich-Schwarz-Zaboronsky (AKSZ) construction \cite{Alexandrov:1995kv} generalizes RSVP as a sigma model. Shifted symplectic geometry \cite{Pantev:2011ns} provides the appropriate derived manifold structure. Path-integral realizations \cite{Cattaneo:1999fm} suggest RSVP can be quantized by AKSZ techniques, with $(\Phi,v,S)$ mapped as fields from $T[1]\Sigma$ into a symplectic dg-manifold $M$.

\section{Fractional Operators and Long-Range Dependence}
Entropy fields $S$ exhibit long-range dependence (LRD). A fractional derivative formulation is natural:
\[
D_t^\alpha S(t) = \frac{1}{\Gamma(1-\alpha)}\int_0^t \frac{\dot S(\tau)}{(t-\tau)^\alpha}\,d\tau, \quad 0<\alpha<1.
\]
This captures persistent memory in cosmological entropy smoothing, consistent with anomalous correlations reported in horizon thermodynamics \cite{Barrow:2020unz}. Spectral methods show $C(\tau)\sim \tau^{-\beta}$, bridging RSVP dynamics with cosmological observations.

\section{RAT Formalism and Autoregressive Cognition}
The Recursive Autoregressive Trajectory (RAT) framework extends RSVP to cognition. State updates follow
\[
x_{t+1} = f(x_t,\Phi_t,v_t,S_t) + \epsilon_t,
\]
with $\epsilon_t$ fractional noise encoding long-range priors. This resonates with predictive processing \cite{Hohwy2013,Friston:2009ze} and autoregressive cognition models \cite{Hinton2006,LeCun2015}. Convergence properties connect to evolutionary replicator dynamics \cite{TaylorJonker1978,HofbauerSigmund1998}.

Semantic infrastructure requires entropy-respecting composition. For modules $\{M_i\}$:
\[
\mathrm{Merge}(\{M_i\}) = \operatorname*{hocolim}_{i\in I} M_i.
\]
Obstructions manifest as sheaf cohomology classes \cite{MacLane1998,BaezStay2010}. The RSVP Emerge functional provides a quantitative constraint:
\[
\mathrm{Emerge}=\int_\Omega \lambda_\Phi\|\Phi_1-\Phi_2\|^2+\lambda_v\|v_1-v_2\|^2+\lambda_S\,\mathrm{KL}(S_1\Vert S_2)\,dx.
\]
This extends Rissanen’s MDL principle \cite{rissanen1978} and Shannon information \cite{Shannon1948,CoverThomas2006} to semantic gluing.
