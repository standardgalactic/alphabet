From the Island of Equations

A memorandum and letter concerning the Relativistic Scalar–Vector Plenum

In an era when every discovery must arrive in the costume of revelation, this project prefers the tone of a ledger. It documents one researcher’s attempt to treat entropy as a field and boredom as a form of precision.

You may read the letter (PDF), or browse the repository for its source files and build scripts.
