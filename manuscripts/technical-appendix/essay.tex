\section*{Preface}

This appendix provides the mathematical scaffolding of the *Relativistic Scalar--Vector Plenum* (RSVP) theory.
It is not meant as proof but as a record of consistency: that the conceptual triad described in the \textit{Island of Equations} and \textit{Introduction to RSVP} can be rendered in formal language without recourse to speculation or metaphor.
The presentation follows the custom of late-twentieth-century field theory, but its tone remains clerical rather than evangelical.

\section{Field Definitions}

Let $\Phi(x^\mu)$ denote a scalar field of informational capacity, $\mathbf{v}(x^\mu)$ a vector field representing local negentropic flow, and $S(x^\mu)$ an entropy density field.  
Together they form the RSVP triad:
\[
\mathcal{R}(x^\mu) = \{\,\Phi,\, \mathbf{v},\, S\,\}.
\]

We define the four-gradient $\nabla_\mu = \partial / \partial x^\mu$ and the spacetime metric $g_{\mu\nu}$ with signature $(+,-,-,-)$.
The theory is formulated on a fixed but non-expanding manifold; gravitation emerges as a curvature of informational gradients rather than a dynamical geometry.

\section{Lagrangian Density}

The minimal Lagrangian capturing the RSVP dynamics is
\begin{equation}
\mathcal{L} = 
\frac{1}{2}\,g^{\mu\nu}\big( \nabla_\mu \Phi \,\nabla_\nu \Phi
+ \lambda\, \nabla_\mu S\,\nabla_\nu S \big)
- \frac{1}{2}\,\alpha\,|\nabla_{[\mu} v_{\nu]}|^2
- \beta\,(\nabla_\mu v^\mu)\,S
- V(\Phi, S),
\label{eq:lagrangian}
\end{equation}
where $\lambda, \alpha, \beta$ are coupling constants and $V(\Phi,S)$ encodes potential interaction terms such as
\[
V(\Phi,S) = \frac{m_\Phi^2}{2}\Phi^2 + \frac{m_S^2}{2}S^2 + \gamma\,\Phi S.
\]
The cross-term $\beta (\nabla_\mu v^\mu)S$ functions as an \textit{entropic source}, allowing the divergence of the vector field to act as a local driver of entropy change.

\subsection{Variational Equations}

Variation of $\mathcal{L}$ with respect to $\Phi, \mathbf{v}$, and $S$ yields:
\begin{align}
\nabla_\mu\nabla^\mu \Phi + \frac{\partial V}{\partial \Phi} &= 0, \label{eq:phi}\\[3pt]
\alpha\,\nabla_\nu \nabla^{[\nu} v^{\mu]} + \beta\,\nabla^\mu S &= 0, \label{eq:v}\\[3pt]
\lambda\,\nabla_\mu\nabla^\mu S - \beta\,\nabla_\mu v^\mu + \frac{\partial V}{\partial S} &= 0. \label{eq:s}
\end{align}

Equations \eqref{eq:phi}--\eqref{eq:s} form the canonical RSVP system.  
They describe coupled diffusive--advective behaviour between capacity, flow, and entropy.
Solutions under stationary boundary conditions reproduce redshift-like attenuation and self-organizing relaxation without reference to cosmic expansion.

\section{Conservation Laws}

From Noether's theorem applied to the symmetries of $\mathcal{L}$, one obtains conserved quantities:
\begin{itemize}
\item \textbf{Information Flux:} $J_\Phi^\mu = \nabla^\mu \Phi$ satisfies $\nabla_\mu J_\Phi^\mu = 0$ in the absence of potential coupling.
\item \textbf{Entropy Continuity:} $\partial_t S + \nabla\!\cdot(\mathbf{v} S) = 0$ when $\beta=0$; otherwise the divergence of $\mathbf{v}$ sources entropy production.
\item \textbf{Energy Functional:} 
\[
E = \int d^3x\, \big[\tfrac{1}{2}(|\nabla \Phi|^2 + \lambda |\nabla S|^2) + \tfrac{\alpha}{2}|\nabla\times\mathbf{v}|^2 + V(\Phi,S)\big],
\]
conserved under time translation symmetry.
\end{itemize}

These quantities provide the operational meaning of “bookkeeping” in RSVP: energy, entropy, and information are not distinct substances but conserved currencies within one ledger.

\section{Entropic Interpretation}

The coupling constant $\beta$ in Eq.~\eqref{eq:lagrangian} measures the degree to which entropy production is sourced by vector divergence.
For $\beta > 0$, regions of convergent flow ($\nabla_\mu v^\mu < 0$) act as local negentropic sinks, stabilizing structure.
For $\beta < 0$, they generate entropic diffusion, mimicking redshift.
This simple term replaces the need for an expanding metric: redshift becomes a scalar consequence of informational dilution.

\section{Relation to Existing Theories}

RSVP may be viewed as a generalization of several preceding frameworks:
\begin{itemize}
\item The \textbf{Einstein--Hilbert action} when $\Phi$ and $S$ are constant, reducing to curvature dynamics.
\item The \textbf{Klein--Gordon field} in the limit $\mathbf{v}\!=\!0$, $\beta\!=\!0$.
\item The \textbf{Navier--Stokes entropy equation} when $\Phi$ is constant and $V\!=\!0$.
\end{itemize}
In this sense, RSVP is not a replacement for standard models but an interpolation among them, parameterized by informational curvature rather than spacetime expansion.

\section{Linearized Solutions}

For small perturbations around equilibrium $(\Phi_0, S_0, \mathbf{v}=0)$, linearization yields coupled wave--diffusion equations:
\begin{align}
\partial_t^2 \delta \Phi - c_\Phi^2 \nabla^2 \delta \Phi &= -\gamma\,\delta S,\\
\partial_t \delta S - D_S \nabla^2 \delta S &= \beta\, \nabla\!\cdot\!\delta \mathbf{v},\\
\partial_t \delta \mathbf{v} - \nu\,\nabla^2 \delta \mathbf{v} &= -\beta\,\nabla \delta S.
\end{align}
Numerical integration of this system exhibits the characteristic RSVP behaviour:  
oscillatory diffusion, entropy-gradient lensing, and long-term relaxation toward finite coherence.
