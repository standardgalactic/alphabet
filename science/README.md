## Core Themes

Across these documents, several key ideas recur:

- **Projection is Lossy**  
  All models discard structure; coherence requires accounting for what is lost.

- **Incompatibility Drives Evolution**  
  Systems evolve not by smoothing contradictions, but by expanding to resolve them.

- **Repair is Fundamental**  
  Stability emerges from recursive constraint repair (CLIO), not static correctness.

- **Identity is Structural**  
  A system’s identity is the persistence of its resolution logic, not its substrate.

- **Closure Defines Completion**  
  Systems are “complete” when they regenerate their own constraints internally.
