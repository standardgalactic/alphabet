\bigskip
\noindent\textbf{Total scenes written so far:} 25 (including interstitial and animal sequences). \textbf{Total scenes planned or proposed:} approximately 45--50. The scaffold is open; further interstitial scenes, animal sequences, and myth-layer fragments may be inserted at any position without disrupting the state topology.

\bigskip
\noindent\textbf{Three active pressure tracks in State II onward:}
(A) Institutional incoherence --- administrative non-convergence, parallel determinations, authority instability.
(B) Animal counterexample --- non-deliberative systems operating within the same spatial and temporal field as human deliberation.
(C) Myth and scriptural contradiction --- variants, retellings, embodied analogues, overheard fragments; never doctrine, never argued.

%------------------------------------------------------------
\section{State I — Fluency}
%------------------------------------------------------------

\noindent\textit{Spherepop operator: Identity $(\Omega_0)$. Maximum channel density. System produces coherence from incoherence without registering cost. Human verbal channel carries everything. Animals peripheral and inert. Perceptual logic at lowest admissible amplitude.}

\bigskip

\noindent\textbf{Scene 1 — The Antechamber} \statuscomplete

\noindent INT. Diplomatic Antechamber --- Day. The film's entry point. Multiple simultaneous conversational channels, overlapping documents with broken and reapplied seals, a translator whose relay subtly alters what it transmits, a servant whose path twice deflects from its intended destination. Rorario stands slightly off-center, his position determined by local pressure rather than intention. A dog sleeps under the table and repositions without being observed. First perceptual flicker: a voice briefly loses semantic weight; a horse's stamp in the courtyard remains audible after the voice restores. Rorario folds one document inside another without fastening them and hands the composite to the document-routing woman who manages the room's actual informational flow without acknowledgment.

\bigskip

\noindent\textbf{Scene 2 — The Superseded Dispatch} \statuscomplete

\noindent INT. Reception Room --- Day. Rorario delivers a response to a letter whose question has already been superseded. Both parties complete the exchange sincerely, neither aware that the reality the letter addressed has changed since its composition. No animals. No perceptual flicker. The cleanest instance of temporal non-termination in the film --- an exchange that is formally complete and referentially void.

\bigskip

\noindent\textbf{Scene 2a --- The Noblewoman} \statuscomplete

\noindent INT. Private Reception --- Day. A noblewoman conducts a diplomatic meeting in place of her indisposed husband, reaching a workable agreement with Rorario. The husband enters briefly, takes a position that contradicts the agreement, and leaves. The noblewoman and Rorario continue without comment, both absorbing the contradiction at the same speed and by the same mechanism. She is not exceptional; she is another mode of managing what the institutional system produces.

\bigskip

\noindent\textbf{Scene 3 --- The Dinner} \statuscomplete

\noindent INT. Formal Dining Hall --- Evening. A diplomatic dinner. A senior diplomat collapses mid-sentence; the event propagates down the table as a quiet relay. A servant is uncertain whether to remove the body before or after the current course. Someone spills wine. A guest's sentence ends in a different grammatical mode than it began. Rorario absorbs the collapsed man's conversational function without moving his seat. The dog at the hall's margin has repositioned without anyone observing the movement. Humor is present and unacknowledged throughout.

\bigskip

\noindent\textbf{Scene 4 --- The Third Position} \statuscomplete

\noindent INT. Administrative Writing Room --- Day. Two contradictory instructions from the same source. Rorario opens both, listens to the clerk's summary of each, and writes a response that proceeds from neither instruction but from the space between them --- a third position that neither document authorized, whose internal consistency is self-sufficient and whose relationship to its sources is one of selective extraction leaving no trace of their mutual incompatibility at the written surface. He seals and dispatches it. End of State I.

\section{State II — Accumulation}
%------------------------------------------------------------

\noindent\textit{Spherepop operator: Three convergent pressure tracks running in parallel. Track 1: geopolitical non-termination (post-Moh\'acs instability). Track 2: perceptual counterexample (animal action within human institutional space). Track 3: institutional incoherence (papal administrative contradictions). Tracks compound progressively toward the saturation point. Perceptual flickers lengthen. Animal presences increase. Myth layer enters. Female characters distributed throughout.}

\bigskip

\noindent\textbf{Scene 3a --- The Stable} \statuscomplete

\noindent EXT. Stable Courtyard --- Morning. Between formal engagements, Rorario passes through the working stable. A farrier works on a horse's hoof; the horse makes continuous micro-corrections to its weight distribution across three legs, each adjustment immediate, executed without the interpolated pauses of self-assessment. The shot holds on the horse fractionally longer than transit strictly requires. First extended animal attention. Perceptual counterexample track opens quietly.

\bigskip

\noindent\textbf{Scene 5 --- The Road} \statuscomplete

\noindent EXT. Roads, Post-Moh\'acs Hungary --- Day. The party moves through politically illegible landscape. A rider ahead halts repeatedly to consult a map that cannot resolve the territory it describes. Oxen work a damaged road section in the same frame, moving through without the map. A dog joins the party's progress from a lateral direction, runs parallel for forty meters, and veers off toward something invisible to the human members without announcement. Geopolitical non-termination track opens.

\bigskip

\noindent\textbf{Scene 6 --- The Document Divergence} \statuscomplete

\noindent INT. Secondary Administrative Chamber --- Late Afternoon. Two clerks produce documents from the same template that are not identical. A third document is found beneath them, bearing the same heading but a different clause structure, already sealed. A fourth document arrives by messenger; it introduces a temporal qualifier making everything provisional. Rorario gathers combinations of documents, reads discontinuously, and issues no instruction. The documents remain in their configuration, unresolved.

\bigskip

\noindent\textbf{Scene 6a --- Outdated Instructions at the Habsburg Court} \statuscomplete

\noindent INT. Official Chamber, Habsburg Administration --- Day. Instructions from Rome, three weeks old, are read aloud. The Habsburg official responds to them as current; Rorario responds to the official as current. Both are sincere. Outside the window, a horse works a water trough --- the same motion, resolved each time. Institutional incoherence and perceptual counterexample tracks co-present for the first time in a single scene.

\bigskip

\noindent\textbf{Scene 7 --- Regensburg} \statuscomplete

\noindent INT. Assembly Chamber, Diet of Regensburg --- Day. The Book of Regensburg under amendment. Theologians --- Contarini's associates, Melanchthon's interlocutors, Bucer's delegates --- argue over a single clause, each amendment opening new contested territory. A spider builds at the window throughout the session, completing before the clause resolves. A dog, partially out of frame, finishes a bone and sleeps before the clause resolves. A monk scratches himself mid-argument. Rorario is present but peripheral. All three pressure tracks simultaneously active for the first time.

\bigskip

\noindent\textbf{Scene 7a --- Street Theater: The Akedah} \statuscomplete

\noindent EXT. Market Square, Regensburg --- Afternoon. A performance company stages the binding of Isaac in the square outside the assembly building. The performer playing the angel arrives slightly late, producing a one-and-a-half second interval during which the knife has begun to descend and the intervention has not yet materialized. The crowd's stillness during this interval differs from the stillness that precedes a known conclusion. The performance resets and repeats for the next audience. Rorario passes through without stopping. The Isaac tradition --- including the midrashic version in which the sacrifice is completed, the ashes remaining, the subsequent restoration --- is carried by the timing of the performance rather than by any statement.

\bigskip

\noindent\textbf{Scene 8 --- The Bubbles} \statuscomplete

\noindent EXT. Village Road --- Day. Rorario's party passes through a village. A girl blows soap bubbles; her dog tracks each one with total commitment, snapping and missing and correcting without deferral. Rorario does not stop. The girl is entirely absorbed in her own activity. The scene is incidental. The bubbles are generated excess with no necessity; the dog's interaction is constrained, selective, convergent. Its importance coheres only retrospectively.

\bigskip

\noindent\textbf{Scene 8a --- Animal Sequence: Dusk} \statuscomplete

\noindent EXT. Roadside --- Dusk. The party halted for the night. A groom attends the horses. An owl calls from the trees; one horse's ear rotates toward the sound with the direction-finding precision of a sensory system calibrated for that function. A mouse crosses the cleared area in four seconds of high-frequency locomotion and disappears into the grass. Rorario holds a document in insufficient light. No dialogue. Pure animal and environmental register.

\bigskip

\noindent\textbf{Scene 9a --- The Farm} \statuscomplete

\noindent EXT. Farmstead --- Day. A woman manages the farm while her husband is away at the Diet, coordinating animals, workers, supply, and decision without the deliberative apparatus of the diplomatic scenes. Rorario's party stops for water and directions; she provides both without pausing her actual work. Something physical and slightly undignified occurs at the yard's edge near the pig enclosure. No one reacts. A system that functions --- not framed as exceptional.

\bigskip

\noindent\textbf{Scene 9 --- The Bridge} \statuscomplete

\noindent EXT. Road and Bridge --- Day. The lead horse stops at the bridge approach and will not cross under applied pressure. The party fords upstream. The bridge is not mentioned again. Later --- several scenes distant, peripheral, not centered --- a sound from that direction. Then nothing. The film does not connect the events.

\bigskip

\noindent\textbf{Scene 10a --- The Falcon} \statuscomplete

\noindent EXT. Hunting Grounds --- Afternoon. The return from the hunt. The handler speaks to the hooded falcon continuously --- commands, assessments, commentary. The falcon responds to prosodic features, pressure, and release, not to semantic content. The adjustments are immediate. The speech is doing something; it is simply not doing what speech between humans does. Rorario rides within earshot. He does not look toward them.

\bigskip

\noindent\textbf{Scene 11 --- The Registry Hall} \statuscomplete

\noindent INT. Ecclesiastical Registry Hall --- Day. Two administrative registers in current simultaneous use, each internally coherent, their determinations systematically divergent. A third clerk copies from a third volume whose content aligns intermittently with each register and with neither. An instruction arrives ordering immediate enforcement; it does not specify which determination to enforce; its phrasing is compatible with both. Documents depart through different exits toward different implementations. No mechanism of reconciliation is invoked. None is absent.

\bigskip

\noindent\textbf{Scene 12 --- The Boundary} \statuscomplete

\noindent EXT. Boundary Zone --- Early Evening. Two officials advance incompatible readings of the same instruction. Rorario steps forward and indicates a point on the ground. A marker is placed. A second group, previously outside the frame, places a second marker at a different location. A dog crosses the interval between the markers without altering its trajectory. Neither marker is erased. The boundary now exists in two locations simultaneously.

\bigskip

\noindent\textbf{Scene 13 --- The Refectorium: David, Elhanan, and the Brother of Goliath} \statuscomplete

\noindent INT. Clerical Refectorium --- Evening. After dinner, several clerics resume (not begin) a discussion of the textual discrepancy between the two accounts of who killed the Philistine champion. The later scribal emendation --- \textit{brother of Goliath} --- is introduced. No version is adopted. A scribe copies the contested name without annotation. The conversation disperses into other topics. The manuscript remains open. The marks in it are not reconciled. Rorario exits without referencing the discussion.

\bigskip

\noindent\textbf{Scene 14 --- The Contracting Contributions} \statuscomplete

\noindent INT. Negotiation Chamber --- Day. A long negotiation. Rorario's contributions progressively shorten: from developed formulations through abbreviated positions to single-clause affirmations to a gesture of continued participation. The others do not notice the reduction. His responses are correct; they are also minimal. This is the saturation point. The central Pop has occurred --- not as an event but as a threshold. No one in the room knows.

\bigskip

\noindent\textbf{Scene 15 --- The Incomplete Dispatch} \statuscomplete

\noindent INT. Writing Room --- Night. Rorario alone, candle, document in progress. He writes a sentence that builds through its necessary subordinate clauses to the point where its central claim must be made. The pen stops. Not crossed out; not finished. He holds the pen. Sets it down. Folds the document along lines that are not its natural fold-lines. Seals it. Places it with the completed dispatches. It will be sent. End of State II --- the Refuse, visible only as a gap.

\bigskip

\noindent\textbf{Scene 15a --- Two Altars} \statuscomplete

\noindent EXT. Rural Clearing --- Late Afternoon. Two stone structures bearing different offerings: one vegetable, one animal. Birds gather on the first, flies on the second. Wind displaces part of the vegetable offering; the birds redistribute. Two figures observe from the clearing's edge. One gestures toward the animal offering; the other looks toward the birds. No words. No agreement or its absence. The Cain/Abel tradition is present as observed phenomena, not as reference. The scene is silent, between State II and III in temporal position.

\bigskip

\noindent\textbf{Scene 15b --- The Spewing Plant} \statuscomplete

\noindent EXT. City Wall, Eastern Quarter --- Morning and Evening. A succulent plant growing in the gap between a city wall's foundation and an adjacent building has extended itself beyond what the available soil and light can sustain. Through the day it flags. By evening it is substantially reduced from its morning condition. No one attends to it. No one names it. The Jonah/gourd tradition is present as a pattern in the world, not as citation.

\section{State III — Writing}
%------------------------------------------------------------

\noindent\textit{Spherepop operator: Collapse toward terminal constraint. Language as residue of eliminated possibilities. Animals inside the same visual grammar as human scenes. Perceptual shifts longer and not always restored. The study is the primary space.}

\bigskip

\noindent\textbf{Scene 16 --- The Manuscript} \statuscomplete

\noindent INT. Study --- Day. The manuscript in advanced but non-final form, its pages accumulated across different sessions, their physical character bearing the evidence of that accumulation. Rorario handles the pages without reading them in any productive sense. A courier passes the window on a horse, indifferent to what is happening inside. A cat enters, investigates a corner, decides against occupation, and leaves. The manuscript remains.

\bigskip

\noindent\textbf{Scene 16a --- Animal Sequence: The Courtyard} \statuscomplete

\noindent EXT. Study Courtyard --- Afternoon. No human figures. A sparrow navigates the courtyard at high resolution, locates something in a crack, extracts it, departs at a diagonal. Two pigeons make their slow circuit. The study window is visible from this angle as an opaque rectangle. From inside the study the courtyard is legible; from the courtyard the study is closed.

\bigskip

\noindent\textbf{Scene 17 --- The Visitor} \statuscomplete

\noindent INT. Study --- Day. A woman --- relative or someone from Rorario's earlier life --- visits. The conversation is warm. At some point during her account her words begin to arrive without the depth of semantic processing that would constitute understanding them fully: the prosodic features continue to be received, the syntactic structures parsed, but the propositional substance fails to elaborate into its inferential extensions. The perceptual shift is not restored until after she has left. The film does not protect her from its formal logic.

\bigskip

\noindent\textbf{Scene 17a --- Isaac's Ashes: A Fragment} \statuscomplete

\noindent INT. Apothecary's Workshop --- Day. Rorario waits for a small commission to be prepared. In an adjacent room, an assistant reads aloud from a text. The wall between the rooms absorbs the middle of each sentence while allowing beginnings and ends to pass through: \textit{the ashes upon the mountain, which were gathered\ldots} / \textit{and God, who restores\ldots} / \textit{as from the heap of what was consumed, the renewed\ldots} The apothecary gathers a pale powder from the work surface into a sealed vessel. The subject of the overheard text is carried as impression, not as content.

\bigskip

\noindent\textbf{Scene 18 --- Terminus} \statuscomplete

\noindent INT. Study --- Late Afternoon. The manuscript has reached its final form through the cessation of addition, not through completion. The pen is at the table's edge, not in the working area. Two dogs settle in the courtyard outside: one immediately, one after a single circuit. Both resolved within the same cut. Rorario looks at the table, the window, nothing in particular. End of State III.

\section{State IV — The Bayle Coda}
%------------------------------------------------------------

\noindent\textit{Spherepop operator: Quotient morphism. Late 17th century. Everything works perfectly. The perceptual instability of States II and III disappears entirely. Full legibility restored, but thinner. The tragedy is that nothing appears to have been lost.}

\bigskip

\noindent\textbf{Scene 19 --- The Printing House} \statusplanned

\noindent INT. Printing House, Amsterdam --- Day. Clean, ordered, efficient. A woman who sets type handles the Rorario manuscript before the compositor does. She reads a passage, sets it down, moves on. No pause. No recognition. A compositor receives the material. Everything works. No struggle, no gap, no visible resistance. Forty pages become eleven lines. The type is clean, justified, complete. Nothing in the medium registers that anything is missing. The compositor does not struggle. He sets what is there, and what is there is already reduced.

\bigskip

\noindent\textbf{Scene 20 --- The Entry} \statusplanned

\noindent INT. Study, Rotterdam --- Day. Pierre Bayle at his desk. The \textit{Dictionnaire historique et critique} in production around him. He consults sources, selects, condenses, discards --- performing in a single gesture what the entire preceding film has treated as a long chain of irreversible operations. The entry is written. It is legible, stable, complete. The provenance is not marked as lost. It simply is not there. The film ends not on absence but on the entry's perfect, indifferent coherence. The tragedy is that nothing appears to have been lost.

\section{Planned Additional Scenes — Not Yet Written}
%------------------------------------------------------------

\noindent The following scenes are proposed insertions distributed across States II and III. They maintain the three-track ratio (approximately 40\% institutional, 30\% animal/environmental, 30\% myth-layer fragments). None are mandatory; all are topologically admissible without disrupting the existing structure.

\bigskip

\noindent\textbf{Scene A --- Tavern Game: Goliath Rotating Roles} \statusnew

\noindent INT. Tavern, Central European City --- Night. A group of travelers plays a competitive game or staging in which the role of the giant-killer rotates between players, each taking the position for a round before passing it on. The game's rules do not resolve which character the player is performing. The game is not about the story; it uses the story as a scoring framework. Laughter and argument about the game's rules rather than about its historical content. The myth layer circulates as a game mechanic rather than as a belief.

\bigskip

\noindent\textbf{Scene B --- Children's Game: Isaac Reset} \statusnew

\noindent EXT. Courtyard or Street --- Day. Children playing a game whose structure involves one child being symbolically ``burned'' or eliminated and then returning to play from the beginning --- reset rather than truly eliminated, the game's rules building in a mechanism for the return of the sacrificed player. The game is absorbed and physical; the children are entirely focused on the game's competitive logic. The Isaac resurrection tradition is present as a play mechanic.

\bigskip

\noindent\textbf{Scene C --- Animal Sequence: Migratory Birds} \statusnew

\noindent EXT. Open Country --- Dawn. A large murmuration or migratory flight. No human figures. The coordination of many thousands of animals moving as a single responsive field, each individual adjusting to its immediate neighbors, the global pattern emerging from local rules without any directing intelligence. Duration: sufficient for the viewer to stop looking for the pattern's origin and begin experiencing it as a field condition. No dialogue. Pure visual and acoustic register.

\bigskip

\noindent\textbf{Scene D --- Animal Sequence: The Ants} \statusnew

\noindent EXT. Stone Wall or Garden --- Day. Close observation of an ant colony's traffic on a stone surface. The paths are established, maintained, and occasionally modified in response to obstacles without any visible deliberation. A piece of food larger than any individual ant is moved collectively. The scene is entirely close-up, the stone filling the frame, the colony's activity constituting the scene's entire content. No human presence. Duration: as long as it takes for the movement to feel inevitable rather than coordinated.

\bigskip

\noindent\textbf{Scene E --- The Puppet Theater} \statusnew

\noindent EXT. or INT. Public Space --- Day. A traveling puppet show stages a biblical or heroic narrative in which the narrated account and the puppets' actions diverge in small but persistent ways --- the narrator describes an action that the puppet is not performing, or a puppet performs an action that the narrator has not described, the two tracks running in parallel without collision or correction. The audience watches both tracks. No one in the crowd remarks on the divergence. The myth layer operates as a formal condition of the medium rather than as an error.

\bigskip

\noindent\textbf{Scene F --- The Incomplete Map} \statusnew

\noindent INT. Cartographer's Workshop or Study --- Day. A map being prepared for a region whose current political configuration differs from the configuration present in the cartographer's source materials. The cartographer works from the sources, producing a map that will be accurate to its sources and inaccurate to the territory it will be used to navigate. He is aware of this. He does not have access to the information that would correct it. He continues. The map is not wrong; it is late.

\bigskip

\noindent\textbf{Scene G --- Animal Sequence: Night Insects} \statusnew

\noindent EXT. Exterior Wall or Garden --- Night. Insects navigating by moonlight or firelight. Close observation of the behavior of moths around a flame or lantern: the characteristic spiral approach, the repeated near-contact, the continuous self-correction that is simultaneously a form of orientation and a form of undoing that orientation. No human figures. The flame is present as a light source, not as a symbol. Duration sufficient to observe the specificity of individual trajectories within the general pattern.

\bigskip

\noindent\textbf{Scene H --- Cain's Sign: An Ambiguous Mark} \statusnew

\noindent EXT. Road or Gate --- Day. A figure with a mark on his face or hand passes through a checkpoint or gate. The guards at the checkpoint respond to the mark with a behavior that is indeterminate between recognition of a protected status and avoidance of a stigmatized one --- their deference could be either. The marked figure passes through without speaking. The ambiguity of what the mark designates, and therefore of what the guards' response signifies, is not resolved by any subsequent scene. The Cain tradition is present as visual ambiguity in the world.

\bigskip

\noindent\textbf{Scene I --- The Jonah Plant: Interior Version} \statusnew

\noindent INT. Room with Window --- Day. A plant on a windowsill, or a vine growing along a wall, visible from a room in which administrative work is being conducted. The plant is not attended to by any character. Over the duration of the scene --- which is occupied primarily by the administrative activity --- the plant loses a leaf, or a branch droops from the heat of the window, or the light shifts in a way that renders it less viable than it was at the scene's beginning. No one notices. The scene continues. The plant's condition has changed. Nothing has been done about it.

\bigskip

\noindent\textbf{Scene J --- The Translator's Error That Is Not Corrected} \statusnew

\noindent INT. Reception or Administrative Chamber --- Day. A translator renders a statement that differs from the original in a way that changes its operative meaning --- not through incompetence but through the structural impossibility of equivalence between the two languages involved at this particular point of the statement. The error is of a kind that could be corrected if the error were identified. The error is not identified. Both parties proceed on the basis of their respective versions. The document produced from the exchange reflects the translated version.

\bigskip

\noindent\textbf{Scene K --- Abel's Altar: An Accidental Version} \statusnew

\noindent EXT. Outdoor Space --- Day. Something falls from an altar or offering table in a way that is indeterminate between accident and significance: a vessel tips, or an ember dislodges, or the arrangement of an offering is disrupted by wind or an animal. The event is witnessed by two or three figures whose interpretations of it differ, none of which is voiced to the others. Each responds to the event from within their own interpretive frame. The responses are incompatible. None is marked as correct. The scene does not attribute cause.

\bigskip

\noindent\textbf{Scene L --- The Boundary Revisited} \statusnew

\noindent EXT. The Boundary Zone from Scene 12 --- Later. The same location, at a different time. Both markers remain. Vegetation has grown around each in ways that are beginning to obscure their surfaces. Activity has accumulated around one marker that suggests it has been treated as authoritative by those immediately adjacent to it; similar but different activity around the other. The two zones of activity do not acknowledge each other. A dog crosses the interval between them, the same trajectory or a similar one. Neither marker has been removed.

\bigskip

\noindent\textbf{Scene M --- The Contracting Letters} \statusnew

\noindent INT. Study, State III --- Day. Rorario composes a letter to an institutional correspondent. The letter is shorter than his letters from State I by a magnitude that is visible if one were comparing them but is not visible within the scene itself. The formulations are correct, economical, and minimal. He seals it without re-reading. A second letter, begun and abandoned, remains on the table --- not crossed out, simply not continued, its half-sentence preserving a thought that has been neither completed nor cancelled.

\bigskip

\noindent\textbf{Scene N --- Animal Sequence: Horses in Rain} \statusnew

\noindent EXT. Open Yard or Field --- Day. Rain. Several horses in an open space, their responses to the rain distributed across the group without coordination: some have turned to face away from the weather, some stand indifferent to it, one has moved to a position of partial shelter. None has been directed. Each has resolved its own relationship to the available conditions. No human figures. The rain continues. The horses continue.

\bigskip

\noindent\textbf{Scene O --- The Play Within: A Staged Argument} \statusnew

\noindent INT. or EXT. Performance Space --- Day or Evening. A theatrical or semi-theatrical entertainment stages a philosophical or judicial argument in which two advocates advance incompatible positions before a judge or arbiter who, at the conclusion of the performance, delivers a verdict that does not correspond to either position advanced but constitutes a third formulation that incorporates elements of each. The audience responds to the verdict with the mix of satisfaction and unease appropriate to resolutions that are formally complete and substantively incomplete. Rorario is present as a spectator. He does not react to the verdict.

\bigskip

\noindent\textbf{Scene P --- Bayle Writing: The Selection Process} \statusnew

\noindent INT. Bayle's Study --- Day. An addition to Scene 20, or a scene preceding it. Bayle consults three different sources on Rorario. Each source contains different information; some of it is inconsistent across sources; some appears in one source only. He reads. He selects. He discards. The discarded material --- letters, references, dates that do not fit the entry's structure --- is set aside without ceremony. The selection process is efficient, practiced, and indifferent to what it does not retain. The entry grows toward its eleven lines.
