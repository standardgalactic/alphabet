“Oh wow / damn, we are in a strange journey,”

“from LessWrong to the universe, a strange idea,”

Glossary

LessWrong — rationalist internet forum/community focused on AI risk, Bayesian reasoning, futurism.

Roko’s Basilisk — famous LessWrong thought experiment involving future AI punishment scenarios.

Neoreaction (NRx) — anti-democratic internet political/philosophical movement.

Grok — likely reference to xAI’s chatbot, though “grok” also means deep intuitive understanding from Robert A. Heinlein’s novel Stranger in a Strange Land.

GitHub — GitHub, code hosting and version-control platform.

Vim — Vim text editor known for keyboard-driven and modal workflows.

WSL — Windows Subsystem for Linux.

AutoHotkey — AutoHotkey keyboard automation and scripting tool for Windows.

Dvorak — alternative keyboard layout designed for efficiency.

QWERTY — standard keyboard layout.

SGA — “Standard Galactic Alphabet,” the glyph script associated with the enchanting table language in Minecraft and broader internet/gaming culture.

EarthCube — possibly a personal/worldbuilding/project reference.

Oblicosm — user-created experimental programming language and semantic-field framework based on admissibility geometry, recursive stabilization, entropy dynamics, and constraint-oriented computation.

Lacan — Jacques Lacan, referenced here through Helen Rollins’s Psychocinema and Lacanian psychoanalysis, especially themes of desire, identity, symbolic structures, and fractured subjectivity.

Pollyanna — Pollyanna (1960), family film associated with optimism, denial, and forced positivity.

The Witch — The Witch (2015), psychological folk-horror film associated with paranoia, repression, and religious dread.

Melancholia — Melancholia (2011), apocalyptic psychological film associated with depression, cosmic fatalism, and emotional collapse.

Superman — likely referring to Man of Steel (2013), associated here with confrontation, responsibility, and difficult recognition rather than naïve optimism.

Keaton — Buster Keaton in the Goat (1921).

Micromegas — Micromégas, Voltaire’s 1752 science-fiction satire about gigantic cosmic beings observing humanity from an alien perspective.

Spherepop — user-created semantic and computational framework centered on bubbles, admissibility, recursive symbolic structures, and semantic-field dynamics.

“الهراء” (harāʾ) — nonsense, bullshit, empty talk.

“طلاسم” (ṭalāsim) — magical symbols, occult glyphs, cryptic writing.

“سورة” (sūra) — chapter of the Qur’an; here used metaphorically for mysterious/formal textual power.
